## Part 5 Writing · General writing

(For reference) In one's life, there are always some essential choices to make, and choosing a major in college is undoubtedly one of them. People usually make decisions based on different principles. Some insist that what major to choose largely depends on one's interest. If the major cannot arouse one's interest, no matter how promising it is, it is not the right choice, and the decision is bound to bring about unpleasant consequences.

However, others have a different opinion. They believe it is reasonable to choose a major by analyzing its prospects, that is, in what way the major can benefit one's personal development and future career after graduation. They think that only in this way can students have a promising future.

Personally, I agree with the former idea. College is a place to accumulate knowledge and experience, and that is not an easy task. The desire to learn is an indispensable foundation for a meaningful and rewarding college life. With interest, persistence, and hard work, students can finally fulfill their dreams and live up to their own expectations.
