## Part 5 Writing · General writing（页07–08）

(For reference) Teenagers are among the heaviest users of cell phones, computers, numerous types of video games, and other electronic devices. Some people say, with the ever increasing amount of time teenagers spend on these electronic devices, they have grown distant from their parents. But others have different opinions. I think the use of electronic devices can have both positive and negative effects on the relationship between children and their parents. On the one hand, the more time teenagers spend on electronic devices, the less time they have to spend with their parents and communicate with them. There are indications that electronic communication may be reinforcing teenagers' peer relationships at the expense of the relationship with their parents. On the other hand, while there is a statistically significant negative correlation between computer use and closeness to parents, some experts find that this is not true with cell phone users. This finding is attributed to the fact that adolescents commonly use cell phones to maintain a good relationship with their parents. Therefore, it is possible that cell phones aid parent-teen closeness as they allow parents and teenagers to stay in touch when they are not together.

## Part 5 Writing · General writing

(For reference) Internet technology has a significant impact on our way of learning, and online classes are becoming the "new normal" for many people. Yet, as with any other learning methods, online learning has its own set of positives and negatives.

To start with, online learning offers us an efficient way of learning with all the tools like videos, PDFs, and podcasts beyond traditional textbooks. Besides, it allows us to attend classes from any location without geographical boundaries. Additionally, online lectures can be recorded, archived, and shared for future reference.

But for many of us, the biggest challenge of online learning is the struggle with focusing on the screen for long periods of time. There is always a great chance for students to be distracted by social media or other sites. Also, we are no longer in the physical company of our peers when studying online, so there is an inevitable sense of isolation.

As for me, I think online learning has presented us with more possibilities and ensured an uninterrupted learning journey in unexpected circumstances. But it can never replace face-to-face classes because the best education is always carried out intensively and in-person. Online learning should remain an option but not the only option.

## Part 3 Translation · Translation

Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) is an important part of traditional Chinese culture. It originated from practice and was enriched and developed continuously through practice. With thousands of years of development, TCM has formed a unique set of treatments and theoretical systems. TCM holds that the human body is made up of yin and yang elements. When there is a balance between yin and yang in the body, the person will have no disease. Meanwhile, TCM regards the individual as a whole. It emphasizes the treatment of the whole body and aims at regulating the balance between yin and yang. Treatments such as the Four Methods of Diagnosis and acupuncture are major components of TCM, and are still widely used today.

## Part 4 Writing · Writing

(For reference) A variety of food safety issues have been extensively reported by the media. For example, expired ingredients were used in restaurants, and foods were cooked with oil that has been used too many times.

The food safety issues have had a negative impact on our life and social development. To begin with, the security of public health has been severely affected. When oil is reused too many times, some of its components can cause cancer, which highlights the severity of the consequences when such oil is used for cooking. Moreover, food safety problems have proved to be an obstacle to the growth of domestic consumption. With increasing concerns about food safety, people cannot buy food at ease, which will undoubtedly affect our economic development.

There are several ways to deal with the food safety issues. First of all, the government should tighten food safety regulations, improve the technical certification and accreditation system, crack down on any breaches, and help the public regain confidence in food products. Besides, companies should be required to strengthen their quality control, discipline themselves, promote a business culture of honesty, and make their contributions to promoting sustainable economic growth.
