5) He restates the thesis and summarizes the main points.
6) Three types of connectives are used.
(a) Transitions
Examples:
"How serious is the problem of academic anxiety in college, and what are its consequences?" (Para. 2)
"Now that we have seen how serious the problem is, let's look at what we can do to overcome the problem." (Para. 3)
(b) Internal preview
Example:
"I will introduce three methods  keeping things organized, making room for fun activities, and developing a healthy lifestyle." (Para. 3)
