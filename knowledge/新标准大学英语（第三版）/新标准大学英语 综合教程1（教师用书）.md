# Unit 1 College: Survive and thrive

## Unit overview

This unit focuses on how to grow as freshmen and aims to help Ss overcome difficulties in the transitional period and live a meaningful life. The diary entries in *Active reading* narrate a U.K. freshman's first week in college. The text in *Further exploration* describes how three freshmen, respectively from Russia, Mexico, and France, overcome their difficulties in social life, studies, and money management. Then the video provides a professor's suggestion that it is important to grow our intelligence rather than prove our intelligence in college. In *Project*, Ss are asked to talk with their partner about challenges they have encountered and offer suggestions to each other. The text in *Extended reading* explains why college is a most rewarding experience in our lives. By learning this unit, Ss will be more prepared to adapt to the new environment and make the most of college life. Hopefully Ss can understand what growing up means for freshmen, and they will have the courage and wisdom to overcome more challenges in the future.

## Scenario

The scenario in this unit can be analyzed from the following aspects:
**Setting:** Participating in an English Club activity
**Topic:** Challenges for freshmen and ways to overcome them
**Identity:** Speakers: College students participating in the English Club activity
**Purpose:** To talk about difficulties from different aspects of college life and give suggestions to meet the challenges

**Teaching suggestions**
T can ask whether Ss have encountered any difficulties in the beginning days of their college life. With some responses, T may say that it is natural for freshmen to face various difficulties in this transitional period because the environment of college is totally different from that of high school. And then T introduces the scenario to Ss and asks them to have a conversation in pairs.

After the tryout, T can ask Ss to have a reflection on the difficulties in the conversation in four aspects: content, language, structure, and communication. T can invite a pair to speak out their difficulties in trying out the scenario and write down their difficulties on the blackboard as an example. Then give Ss three minutes to fill in the table with their own difficulties. After that, T can naturally point out the learning objectives of this unit, which can help Ss be clear about what they will learn and what they will be able to do upon completion of this unit.

## Active reading

The text includes six diary entries written by a U.K. freshman. In these diary entries, the author details challenges from different aspects of college life in the first week. After learning this text, Ss may realize the similarities of the challenges facing them and the author in this transitional period. Meanwhile, Ss may recognize their differences resulted from different cultural backgrounds. They will be more open to difficulties in their life and learn to grow up by overcoming these emerging problems. Ss will learn useful expressions to describe their own situations and learn how to use the writing technique of exaggeration to emphasize what is intended to be stressed in the narration.

### Before you read

**Teaching suggestions**
This is a warm-up activity to relate Ss' personal experiences to the content of the text. After Ss write down challenges they have met, T can invite Ss to share their answers. The list of challenges can be added to be as much inclusive as possible.

**Reference answers**
- Managing my money
- Balancing between study and social activities
- Disciplining myself
- Choosing which clubs to join in
- Washing clothes by hand
- Living in the dormitory
- Getting along with the roommates

**Additional activity**
For an alternative choice, the class can do a "Guess and check" activity. T can design a questionnaire to survey challenges Ss are encountering at the very moment. Each Ss inputs their answers. After inviting Ss to guess what are the most common challenges in class, T can show the result and comment on the reasons accountable for the most common ones.

### Diary of a fresher

### Text interpretation

本文展现了一名英国的大一新生初入大学第一周写下的六篇日记，依次呈现了住宿、见导师、食堂就餐、听课、交朋友、去图书馆以及清洗衣物等不同侧面的大学生活，反映了全新的环境带给大学新生的诸多挑战。日记内容与学生目前所处的情境具有极大的相似性，能够使他们联想到自己的生活经历，产生强烈的代入感，学生对于日记中描述的问题也会比较感兴趣。

课文传递了一种积极的成长观：挑战是新生活的一部分，成长就意味着要面对困难、解决问题，这是每个人不同阶段都要面临的必修课。日记中诸如同伴压力、照顾自己生活的辛苦等都跃然纸上，让学生意识到自己生活中遇到的诸多问题也是其他同龄人正在经历的，不必为此感到惊慌。需要做的是积极调整心态，找到解决问题的办法，尽快适应大学生活。教师可以引导学生分析日记中反映的大学新生挑战，结合自己的实际情况谈谈遇到的相似或者不同的挑战，在现实与课本的互动中建立直面困难的信心，帮助学生增长本领，磨砺品性，成为德智体美劳全面发展的社会主义建设者和接班人。

日记的整体风格简洁轻快，特色鲜明。作者在叙事中融入细腻的心理活动，通过生动的描写来呈现大学新生活的立体和多面。学生可以通过理解练习 1 和 2 来深入理解课文，把握主旨内容。同时，作者擅长使用夸张的修辞手法，使描述更加幽默。学生可以结合 Analyzing writing techniques 部分的讲解以及练习进一步挖掘文中使用的夸张的例子，分析达成的效果。学生在最后产出时可以尝试使用夸张的修辞手法。

### Culture notes

1 Fresher
A fresher is a student in their first year at university, especially a new arrival. The word "fresher" emphasizes that students are new arrivals; "freshmen" (*AmE*) refers to students in their first year at university. Some other terms are:

| Year of study | British expression | American expression |
|---------------|--------------------|---------------------|
| first year | first-year student / fresher (in first months of study) | freshman |
| second year | second-year student | sophomore |
| third year | third-year student / final-year student (of a three-year programme) | junior |
| fourth year | fourth-year student / final-year student (of a four-year programme) | senior |

2 Tutor
A tutor in the U.K. is a teacher or lecturer who has particular responsibility for a small group of students (a personal tutor who sees particular tutees). A tutorial is a period of teaching in a university which

（页底截断，接下页）

emphasizes critical discussion between one student, or a small group, and a tutor. Generally this is on a prepared topic, and sometimes students bring prepared writing (notes or a draft essay) and make a presentation to the tutor and other students, which is then discussed.

3 Freshers' fair
A freshers' fair is largely arranged by university faculties, student societies and clubs to give information to new students in their first week at university, and to attract new members. Often faculties, departments, societies and clubs will set up a table or tent on campus where they display the information about the faculty, society or club, and about how they are able to help students. They give out information packs to students. Sometimes they also give students small gifts or discount vouchers for nearby restaurants, pubs, or shops. For new students in the U.K., there is usually an induction week when they receive a lot of information about their courses.

4 College major
In U.S. colleges, students choose their majors, which means the main subject areas they specialize in. They might also choose a minor, which is the subject that they study in addition to the major. The system in U.K. universities is very different. Students apply for a course and study a subject. While some U.K. universities may allow for some flexibility in course selection, there isn't typically a concept of minors or majors. Instead, a student might take core modules and select options to build a well-rounded education within their field of study. In the U.K., a student is not called "an English literature major," but "an English literature undergraduate."

### Language points

1 **After a tiring car journey, we arrive at my hall of residence for me to get settled in.** (Para. 1)
**Notes:** A "hall of residence" is a college or university building where students live. In American English, it is called a dorm, dormitory, or residence hall.

2 **My room is five floors up, and the lift has a sign on it, "Out of order."** (Para. 1)
**Meaning:** My room is on the fifth floor. But there is a sign on the lift, saying that the lift is not working. (So the author and her parents have to climb up to the fifth floor. That is why the author's mother is flushed and gasping for breath.)
**out of order:** if a machine or piece of equipment is out of order, it is not working （机器或设备）发生故障的，失灵的
The washing machine has been out of order for weeks and nobody has it repaired.

3 **Finally, with my mother flushed and gasping for breath, we find the room and walk in.** (Para. 1)
**Notes:** The structure "with my mother flushed ..." is an absolute construction, denoting the condition of the author's mother when they find the room.

4 **After one minute, my father climbs out.** (Para. 2)
**Notes:** The author uses the expression "climb out" to indicate that the room is too small. It is so small that one needs to "climb in and out" instead of "walk in and out." Here the author is exaggerating, as in some of the coming paragraphs.

5 **The room is barely big enough for one, ... I can stretch out full-length on the bed and touch three walls without moving a muscle.** (Para. 2)
**Meaning:** The room is too small. ... When I lie on the bed, I can touch three walls even when I am not moving my body. The author is using exaggeration to say how small the room is.
**barely:** *ad.* in a way that almost does not happen, exist, etc. 仅仅；勉强地；几乎没有
The roads were barely wide enough for two cars to pass.
**stretch out:** lie down, usu. in order to sleep or rest 躺下（睡觉或休息）
After a long day's work, she likes to stretch out on her bed to rest after a hot bath.
**full-length:** *ad.* sb. who is lying full-length is lying flat with their legs straight out 脚伸直地；平躺地
She was lying full-length on the grass.

6 **There's a coffee morning for freshers. I meet my tutor, a tall man with sloping shoulders, who is making a determined effort to be friendly.** (Para. 4)
**"Have you come far?" he asks as he peers down at me. As he speaks, his head jerks wildly from side to side, which makes his coffee spill out.** (Para. 5)
**Meaning:** In these two paragraphs, the author gives a vivid physical description of the tutor, who is uncomfortable in the situation and doesn't know how to act. He's trying to be relaxed and friendly, but it doesn't come naturally.
**peer:** *vi.* look very carefully at sth., esp. because you are having difficulty seeing it （尤指因看不清而）盯着看，凝视
He peered at the tag to read the price.

7 **"Splendid!" he says and moves on to the fresher standing beside me. "Have you come far?" he asks. "Splendid!" he continues, without waiting for the answer, and moves on.** (Para. 7)
**Meaning:** The tutor asks the same question and gives the same response to the students' answers. He moves on to the next student without even waiting to hear the previous student's answer. This indicates that the tutor is a little nervous and behaves in an unnatural way.

8 **Am feeling a bit hungry, and I feel like I haven't eaten for three days.** (Para. 9)
**Notes:** "Am feeling a bit hungry" is not a complete sentence and the subject "I" is omitted. Here the author has used a free writing style, which is ungrammatical. In formal writing, it is suggested to use complete sentences.

9 **That's impressive! I feel so ignorant ... I don't even feel worthy of breathing the same air as her.** (Para. 17)
**Meaning:** This sentence is exaggerated and humorous, emphasizing the gap between the author and Sophie – Sophie seems much more intelligent, knowledgeable, and enthusiastic than the author. In comparison, the author feels so ignorant that she thinks she doesn't deserve to breathe the same air as Sophie.

10 **Mum reassures me that it's mine for as long as I need it ...** (Para. 20)
**reassure:** *vt.* make sb. feel calmer and less worried or frightened about a problem or situation 使安

（页底截断，接下页）

心；使放心；使消除疑虑
The salesman reassures me that the machine is safe to use.
**as long as:** used to say that one thing can happen or be true only if another thing happens or is true 只要
My parents don't care what college I go to as long as I'm happy.

11 **For some reason, I also have to swear that I won't break the library rules, and if I do, I'll be sent to prison.** (Para. 21)
**Meaning:** Again, this is humor through exaggeration. All libraries have rules, but those who break the rules are unlikely to go to prison. The expression "for some reason" indicates that the author doesn't understand why she needs to swear to follow the library rules.
**swear:** (*swore*, *sworn*) *vt.* promise that you will do sth. 起誓保证（做某事）
He swore that he would never see her again.

12 **(What!? For speaking too loudly?)** (Para. 21)
**Notes:** The sentence is in brackets. This is what the author thinks to herself. She does not say it out loud.

13 **I'm not sure what happens to my dirty clothes after putting them in the clothes basket and before finding them clean, ironed, and ready to be worn again.** (Para. 22)
**Meaning:** This is a humorous reference to the fact that at home, the author's mother does all of the washing for the author. Hence the author claims not to know what happens to the clothes.

### Analyzing writing techniques

**Teaching suggestions**
T can write a sentence "The room is small" on the blackboard and then write another sentence "I can stretch out full-length on the bed and touch three walls without moving a muscle." By inviting Ss to tell the difference between the two sentences, T introduces the writing technique of exaggeration and lead Ss to learn about the functions and usage of it.

1
**Reference answers**
**Sunday:** After one minute, my dad climbs out.
**Monday:** As he speaks, his head jerks wildly from side to side ...
**Wednesday:** I don't even feel worthy of breathing the same air as her.
**Friday:** I also have to swear that I won't break the library rules, and if I do, I'll be sent to prison.

2
**Reference answers**
Sunday: After one minute, my dad climbs out.
The author uses the phrase "climb out" to mean there is not enough room for the father to walk out. This exaggeration can give readers a vivid picture and leave them a deep impression.

Monday: As he speaks, his head jerks wildly from side to side ...
The tutor is very nervous in the first meeting. The author describes the tutor as "jerks from side to side" in an exaggerating way, which makes the image of a nervous tutor stands there fresh and vivid.

Wednesday: I don't even feel worthy of breathing the same air as her.
In normal sense, all individuals can breathe the air freely. With the words "I don't even feel worthy of breathing the same air as her," the author wants to emphasize how ignorant she is compared to the frighteningly intelligent Sophie. By using exaggeration, the author has directly shown she is unconfident and depressed with peer pressure.

Friday: I also have to swear that I won't break the library rules, and if I do, I'll be sent to prison.
Rationally speaking, it is not possible to put a college student into prison only because they break the library rules. By exaggerating the disastrous consequence, the author has made it clear that the library rules are quite strict.

## Further exploration

After sorting out what is challenging freshmen in the transitional period in *Active reading*, Ss will learn how to solve the problems faced by them. The text describes how three freshmen in different countries overcame their difficulties. T can lead Ss to analyze why these solutions are effective in specific situations. ### Settling down around the world

### Language points

1 **But then I realized I was missing out on so many other things at college. I talked to Mom, and she suggested I join a club.** (Para. 2)
**miss out on:** not have the chance to do sth. that you enjoy and that would be good for you 错失机会
If you don't join the summer camp, you will miss out on so much fun.
**Notes:** "Mom" is American English. An alternative spelling is "Mum," which is British English.

2 **But I ended up concentrating on writing rather than listening.** (Para. 3)
**Meaning:** But the result was that I was so busy writing that I had no extra attention paid to listening and understanding.

3 **I now appreciate how important it is to distinguish between important information and minor details.** (Para. 4)
**distinguish:** *v.* recognize and understand the difference between two or more things or people 区分；辨别
It is sometimes not easy to distinguish bad seeds from good ones.

4 **My grant for the whole semester would arrive at the start of each semester all at once, so I felt like a millionaire!** (Para. 5)
**grant:**
*n.* [C] an amount of money given to sb., esp. by the government, for a particular purpose （尤指政府发给的）补助金，拨款
If you want to apply for a student grant, please fill in the form.
*vt.* (*fml.*) give sb. sth. or allow them to have sth. that they have asked for 给予；准予
Our government has granted the request from the neighbor country for international aid.
**all at once:** together, at the same time 一起；同时
Three mid-term exams come all at once next Monday.

5 **Initially, I tried a monthly budget, but it didn't work.** (Para. 6)
**initially:** *ad.* at the beginning 起初

He played violin initially, but switched to the piano when he was in his twenties.

6 **Then, I resorted to a weekly budget, but my spending was always out of control.** (Para. 6)
**resort to sth.:** do sth. bad, extreme, or difficult because you cannot think of any other way to deal with a problem 采取，诉诸（不好的方法）
Resorting to violence should not be allowed at any time.

7 **Finally, I put together a daily budget.** (Para.6)
**Meaning:** At the end I made a plan of money management on a daily basis.
**put sth. together:** prepare or produce sth. by collecting pieces of information, ideas, etc. （通过收集资料、观点等）整理出，拼凑
Let's put together an agreement based on the content of the meeting.

**What is the point of college?**

### Scripts

1 From the day we were born, we are told to do well in school. We are told: Study hard. Get good grades! Go to college! And somewhere along the way, we get the idea that our grades are critically important. We celebrate the As. We punish the Cs and Ds. And at some point, we learn to see ourselves as "A students" or "C students." We let the grades define us. We think, "We are A students. We must be inherently intelligent." "If we are C students, we must be inherently not as smart." This affects how we school (study). We stop studying to learn something new. Rather, we study to maintain a GPA. We fear tests as ways to weed us out, and believe teacher comments are point-by-point reasons why we are dumb. We start looking for the easy teachers, and the easy classes, the ones that won't threaten our GPAs. We learn that struggle is a sign of deficiency, (and) that if you have to work hard there must be something wrong with you. And we go to college with the belief that success, failure, worth are all measured by grades.

2 That stops today. The point of college isn't to prove your intelligence, but to grow your intelligence. And our intelligence can only grow when we are challenged. We shouldn't fear tests, or teacher comments, or grades. None of those things define us. They are not even about us. They're all about the work. They tell us "This is where you are right now," not "This is all you'll always be" or "This is who you inherently are." If we get As in a class but learn nothing, we're no smarter than if we didn't take the class at all. Because when we struggle, that's when we learn. And struggling in college means we're doing college right. We have to challenge ourselves. We have to take the hard classes. We have to embrace the hard work, not as a vice, but as a virtue. We have to be open to failure. If we try, do poorly, read the comments, and learn what didn't work, our intelligence grows. And that's the point of college: to grow.

3 The point is not to get As, but to learn from the Bs and Cs and Ds and Fs. And in the end, the grades don't tell you how successful you'll be. And they can't measure your worth. What matters is whether you're learning. So, celebrate the learning. Celebrate the growth. Take the challenging class. Read the comments. Embrace the process. Grow your intelligence.

### Culture notes

1 **GPA**
GPA is short for grade point average which means the average of a student's marks over a period of time in the U.S. education system. GPA is of great significance for college students because it is needed in many situations, e.g., applying for scholarships, pursuing a master's degree, or seeking for a job.

2 **The U.S. grading system**
The U.S. grading system has two types, including the letter and number system. The letter grading system ranges from A to F, and which letter you get depends on a percentage that indicates the number of questions a student has correctly answered. Specifically, A stands for "Excellent" with a percentage of 90%-100%, B for "Very good" with a percentage of 80%-89%, C for "Improvement needed" with a percentage of 70%-79%, D for "Close fail" with a percentage of 60%-69%, and F for "Fail" with a percentage of 0%-59%. The letter system can be further divided into various grade minus and grade plus. The number

### Language points

1 **And somewhere along the way, we get the idea that our grades are critically important. (Para. 1)**
**along the way:** during a process or experience, or during sb's life 在这一过程（经历）中；在生活道路上
*You will realize that love is one of the most important thing along the way.*
**critically:** *ad.* in a way that is very important 极为重要地；很大程度上
*I feel a little nervous in the critically important interview.*

2 **We fear tests as ways to weed us out, ... (Para. 1)**
**weed sb. / sth. out:** get rid of people or things that are not very good 除去，淘汰（不合格的人或物）
*Certain requirements are set to weed some candidates out in this competition.*

3 **They are not even about us. They're all about the work. (Para. 2)**
**Meaning:** All these tests, teacher comments or grades are not for judging us, but for assessing the work.

4 **If we get As in a class but learn nothing, we're no smarter than if we didn't take the class at all. (Para. 2)**
**Notes:** "No / not + a comparative + than" are both correct. The slight difference is that "no + comparative + than" denies the two subjects that are being compared. For example, "no smarter than" indicates that "both of the two are not smart". The sentence shows that it is not advisable to attend a course just for a high grade. If you learn nothing from the course, there is no meaning.

5 **And struggling in college means we're doing college right. (Para. 2)**
**Notes:** "Doing" means performing an action or activity and it can be used in connection with many objects, like doing sports and doing gardening. Here, "doing college" means studying in college.

6 **We have to embrace the hard work, not as a vice, but as a virtue. (Para. 2)**
**Notes:** The structure "not ... but ..." is used to deny one thing while at the same time to favor the other, with the focus on the favored one. In this sentence, the speaker emphasizes that embracing the hard work should be regarded as a virtue.

**Why is college the best time of your life?**

### Language points

1 **However, how can this be true with all that students have to deal with, from the worry of leaving your school routine and childhood friends to the stress of studying for important exams? (Para. 1)**
**Meaning:** Students have to deal with a lot of worries and stress. How can you say that college is the best time of a person's life?
**Notes:** In the structure of "all that ...", "all" is the antecedent (先行词) and "that" is the relative pronoun (关系代词) in the attributive clause (定语从句). The structure of "from ... to ..." serves as the appositive (同位语) of "all," provides more information about the things that students have to cope with in college.

2 **... your self-reliance is no doubt tested even further. (Para. 4)**

*The dessert of choice for the party is ice cream.*

**gain:**
v. gradually get more and more of a quality, feeling, etc., esp. a useful or valuable one 逐渐获得（有用或宝贵的品质、感觉等）

vt. obtain or achieve sth. you want or need 获得，赢得，取得（想要或需要的东西）

**beneficial:** *a.* having a good effect 有利的；有帮助的
*The two companies have reached an agreement that is mutually beneficial.*

**Notes:** "Pick up" can be used before "jobs" and the phrase means to take jobs.

7 **You can meet lots of new people, some of whom will become your friends for life. (Para. 7)**
**Notes:** "Some of whom ..." is a non-restrictive attributive clause (非限定性定语从句), modifying "new people" in the first sentence. It provides extra information to explain some of the new people students meet in college will become life-long friends.

8 **College is a melting pot of people – with different nationalities, cultural beliefs, and points of view. (Para. 7)**

## Unit overview

This unit focuses on the importance of language and language learning and aims to help Ss reflect on their language learning experiences and gain an in-depth understanding of the values of language and language learning. The text in *Active reading* uses four metaphors to explain the roles that language and language learning play in terms of society, history, culture, and thinking. ### Culture notes

**1　Metaphor**

In linguistics, metaphor has both a narrow and a broad sense. In a narrow sense, metaphor (暗喻) means an imaginative way of describing something by referring to something else without using a marker like "like" and "as," e.g., "My love is a red rose." The opposite is a simile (明喻), e.g., "My love is like a red rose." In a broad sense, metaphor includes both the narrow sense and simile.

**2　John Donne**

John Donne is often considered as one of the greatest poets in Britain. "No man is an island" is a well-known

（页底截断，接下页）

line taken from his poem "For Whom the Bell Tolls," which explores the themes of life, death, and the human condition. The first part of the poem says: "No man is an island, entire of itself. Each is a piece of the continent, a part of the main." It uses the metaphor of island and continent to explain the relationship between individuals and humankind.

**3　Rita Mae Brown**

Rita Mae Brown, born in 1944, is an American writer best known for her autobiographical novel. The original quote cited by the text is "Language is the road map of a culture. It tells you where its people come from and where they are going." It highlights the importance of a language in reflecting the past and the future of the people speaking it.

**4　Ludwig Wittgenstein**

Ludwig Wittgenstein is a British philosopher and logician. The famous quote "The limits of my language mean the limits of my world" is taken from his masterpiece Treatise on Logic and Philosophy. This statement highlights the importance of language for thinking.

### Analyzing writing techniques

**Teaching suggestions**

It's important to help Ss understand that metaphor is not only a rhetorical device but also a way of understanding and explaining something abstract. Linguistically, a metaphor is not just one single sentence but can develop into a series of sentences. T can use the metaphor "life is a journey" as an example. In this metaphor, we use the concrete concept "journey" to explain the abstract concept "life." The concept "journey" contains a wide range of elements – "passenger," "departure," "destination," "direction," "crossroad," and "vehicle." We can use these elements to create more metaphorical sentences to illustrate our understanding of life. For example, "there is no return ticket for the journey of life" uses the elements "direction" and "vehicle" to illustrate the idea that you cannot regret and go back to the past. "If life is a journey, then I prefer group travel to independent travel" is also a metaphorical sentence bearing the metaphor "life is a journey." It uses the element "passenger" to express the preference for experiencing life together with other people rather than all by oneself.

### Culture notes

**1 Gendered nouns and definite articles of French and German**

In French, nouns are divided into two categories (masculine and feminine), and different definite articles are used before them. For example, *le* is used before masculine singular nouns, e.g., *le garçon* (the boy); and *la* is used before feminine singular nouns, e.g., *la fille* (the girl). German nouns have three genders (masculine, feminine, and neutral). The gender of German nouns can also be identified by the definite article they take: *der* for masculine singular nouns (*der Bruder*, meaning the brother), *die* for feminine singular nouns and all plural nouns (*die Mutter*, meaning the mother, *die kinder*, meaning the children,) and *das* for neuter singular nouns (*das Museum*, meaning the museum). It's worth noting that grammatical gender categories aren't based on human gender. Complicated rules are employed to determine the gender of nouns. Therefore, for learners of French or German, gendered nouns can be difficult to grasp.

As the video mentions, the French word *chat* (cat) is considered masculine, so it should be *le chat* (the cat). In German, young humans and animals are often considered neutral in gender, so it should be *das Baby* (the baby).

**2 False friends**

False friends are common in European languages that use the same Latin alphabet. For example, the Italian word *libreria* looks like the English word "library," but it means bookstore. In German, *Bad* means "hot spring water" although it looks and sounds like "bad" in English.

1 语言是我们生活中如此自然而又不可或缺的部分，以至于我们常常把语言视为理所当然。也许现在是时候反思一下，想想语言是什么，以及学新的语言意味着什么。

2 语言是桥梁，连接人、国家和文化。正如约翰·多恩所说："没有任何人是一座孤岛。"人隶属于一个群体，才能蓬勃发展；若是彼此孤立，则会陷入困境。也没有任何国家是一座孤岛。在现代世界尤其如此。世界各地的人需要交流资源、产品、技术和思想。没有共同的语言，人和国家都会彼此孤立，无法沟通。

3 英语作为全球通用语，是连接当今世界的巨大桥梁。世界各地的人都可以使用英语进行国际交流。但是，我们也应该学习其他语言，因为世界上有六千多种语言。正如纳尔逊·曼德拉指出的："用一个人听得懂的语言和他交谈，触动的是他的思维；用一个人的母语和他交谈，触动的是他的心灵。"为了能有更多的心灵沟通，我们需要建造更多的桥梁。

4 语言也是窗口，可以让我们窥见历史。学 press 这个英语单词时，你有没有想过为什么它既有"挤压"的意思，又有"印刷、出版书籍的公司"的意思？要解开这个谜，你需要穿越到十五世纪的欧洲。在当时，最早由中国发明的活字印刷术已经在欧洲被改造。一名叫约翰尼斯·谷登堡的德国金匠改良了一种通过"挤压"葡萄来酿酒的装置，从而研制出了欧洲版的活字印刷术，而那种装置的名字就叫 press（挤压器）。谷登堡把这个装置用在印刷机上，把纸铺在上了墨的活字上，再用这个装置来压平。谷登堡印刷机在欧洲具有革命意义，因为它，书籍的价格得以降低，大量印刷书籍成为可能。这很快在欧洲大陆带来了一场思想大爆炸。

5 用美国作家丽塔·梅·布朗的话说，语言还是文化的地图。布朗认为，语言可以告诉我们使用该语言的民族从何处而来。从英语的谚语"趁着晴天晒干草"，我们可以知道英格兰人民过去在传统农庄饲养牲畜的情形。由于当时人们要割草、晒草作为牲畜的饲料，于是就有了这个谚语。现在，它的意思是"把握住稍纵即逝的机会"。在游牧社会，同样的意思可能用不同的情形表示，比如蒙古语会说"趁热吃羊肉"。在阿拉伯社会，人们会说"可以把骆驼带到河边，但是无法强迫它饮水"。英语中有一个特别相似的谚语，但是用的动物是马。受农业传统的深刻影响，汉语则会用牛来表达类似的意思。

6 语言就像眼镜，我们透过它来看待世界。学习一门新的语言，就是用一种新的视角看待世界。当非中文母语者学习用"上火"、"肝火"、"胃火"等汉语词汇来描述溃疡和痤疮时，也是在用阴阳和五行（即金、木、水、火、土）理论来理解世界和人体。这是传统中国医学和中国哲学的基础。同样，当你知道在英语中大学的第一周叫作"迎新会"（orientation），而毕业仪式则叫作"新开始"

### Further exploration

**多语人士的秘诀**

1 我喜欢学多种外语，目前正在学第八门语言。我就是你们所说的多语人士。人们常常问我："你的秘诀是什么？"我总是回答："就是喜欢学语言啊！"但是，他们对这个答案总是不满意。他们总想知道，为什么他们花了好多年学一门语言，却还是无法流利使用。这也引起了我的思考。其他多语人士是如何学会多门语言的呢？我们有哪些共同之处？

2 在一个语言爱好者的集会上，我遇到了来自爱尔兰的本尼。他的方法是从第一天就开始用所学的语言在真实的情境中说话。他从一本旅行手册上学了几句意大利语，然后就开始去找母语人士见面聊天了。他毫不担心会犯错，因为对方的反馈可以帮助他学习。

3 当然，我们并不总是能面对面地见到母语人士。但是，有了网络，我们就更容易找到母语人士，能舒舒服服地在客厅里通过屏幕与他们对语。

4 来自巴西的卢卡斯就是这样学俄语的。他在社交平台上与一百多个说俄语的用户建立联系。然后，他打开其中一个人的聊天框，用俄语打了个"嗨"。对方用俄语回复："嗨，你好吗？"卢卡斯复制这句话，粘贴到和另一个人的聊天框中，这个人又用俄语回复："我很好，谢谢，你呢？"卢卡斯又复制了这句俄语，用来回复第一个人。因为重复了很多次这种对话，很快他就弄懂了俄语聊天常用的开场白。真是一个很巧妙的办法！

5 很明显，每个多语人士都会使用不同的方法习得一门外语，但是他们用的都是他们自己喜欢的方法。学习新语言会让他们获得乐趣，因此付出了努力也不会觉得枯燥。他们会做五颜六色的语法图表、单词卡片，甚至用外语做菜谱，这些都会给他们带来乐趣。我也是这样。学习西班牙语一段时间后，我就决定读我最喜欢的小说《哈利·波特》的西班牙语版本。我已经读过很多次英文版。当然，刚开始我几乎什么都读不懂，但是我坚持阅读。最后，我读西班牙语版本的《哈利·波特》几乎没有什么困难了。

6 所以，多语人士的秘诀是什么呢？找到能使你感受到快乐的语言学习方法。然后，你或早或晚都能流利使用新的语言。

### Extended reading

**遗忘母语**

1 没有人提醒我遗忘自己的母语——汉语——会有多痛苦。这是我和父母能够交流的唯一语言。当我慢慢忘记如何使用汉语，我也失去了与父母交流的能力。

2 我们每周会打一次电话，重复一样的台词。"你吃了吗？"父亲用汉语问。然后就是一段漫长的沉默。"还没有，你们呢？""我答道。""怎么还没吃？都这么晚了！"母亲插话进来。又是长时间的静默。"记得多喝水，出门戴口罩，"母亲继续说。"好，你们也是。"更长时间的静默。"那我们不打扰你了。"对话总是很短，而且很表面。

3 我回家时，我们相处的日子同样让人失望。我有那么多话想说，但是总找不到合适的词，因为长久以来，我一直重视英语，忽视了汉语。每回一次家，我就感觉更空虚一些，就好像我不仅失去了和父母的联系，也失去了我的一部分文化遗产。如果我几乎都不会说汉语了，还能称自己为华人么？

4 我在纽约市出生，孩提时代大多数时候都说汉语，因为我的父母当时（现在也是）只会说这一种语言。二十世纪八十年代，他们从中国移民来到美国。在餐馆和工厂的工作使他们没有时间学习英语。

5 在曼哈顿的唐人街，我身边的人都说汉语。在一些特殊的日子里，他们会聚在一起打麻将，我则能够吃到自己最喜欢的用豆腐做的菜。在家里，我们会看一部流行的电视剧《西游记》，听中文流行歌。上学之前，我所有的朋友都是华人移民的孩子，他们也都说汉语。我生活的方方面面都被汉语包围。

6 进入小学我才开始学英语，在英语作为第二语言的课堂的帮助下，我很快就能说两门语言，在两种语言之间来回切换。父母鼓励我好好上英语课，因为他们相信这是我在美国获得成功的关键，尽管他们自己从来没学过英语。

7 随着时间流逝，汉语在我的生活中扮演的角色越来越不重要。我离家上大学后，就更少听到汉语了。开始第一份工作后，我偶尔会说一下。到了现在，作为一个离家几千里的成年人，我几乎不再说汉语。除了在电话里迁就父母，汉语在我的生活中不再有任何功能。

8 当我发现自己的汉语在退化，为时已晚。首先，我不会方向词了。应该说"左"的时候，我会说"右"。爸爸开车我指路时，这会导致他拐错方向。还有，颜色词我也不会了。"我喜欢你这条绿裙子，"我用汉语对妈妈说。"傻瓜，这是蓝色的！"她大笑道。

9 很多移民的孩子都面临这种状况。要在美国获得"成功"，我们必须掌握一门新语言，放弃自己的母语——我们的父母最擅长的语言。但是，如果父母和子女共同使用的语言被遗忘，他们之间的情感纽带也会变弱。我多么希望能与他们分享我生命中那么多重要的时刻——我曾经获得的奖项、我职业上的变化、能够让他们感到骄傲的场合。这些，我都找不到词语来表达。我和父母没有了交心的对话，没有了对彼此的理解。

10 我妈妈六十四岁生日那天，我意识到自己不能带着我们之间的这个语言障碍过完余生。我决定重新学习汉语，并最终重建我们之间的关系。我尝试与我能找到的所有说汉语的人对话。我开始听中文歌手的歌曲。我开始看汉语电影，重复每一句台词，直到声调都读对。

11 最重要的是，我会给父母打电话，磕磕绊绊地完成一些更有意义的对话。我们每周的通话变得更生动了。我准备了一大堆话题，还有更多的耐心。虽然说汉语时我不再感觉自然，但是它永远都将是我的母语。

# Unit 3 Taste life!

## Unit overview

This unit focuses on the topic of food and aims to help Ss recall their personal experience and explore the cultural significance of a local Chinese food, thus to spread Chinese food culture to the international audience. The text in *Active reading* narrates how a small boy's initial reluctance to eat an oyster shifts to surprise after trying it for the first time, with his father's persuasion. The text in *Further exploration* introduces how street food is a cultural representation of unique flavors and local traditions, distinct from commercial fast food. The video gives special attention to the very popular Chongqing hotpot symbolizing inclusiveness, sharing, and getting together in Chinese culture. In *Project*, Ss are asked to work in a team to make a presentation introducing a local Chinese food including their personal experience and the cultural meaning of it. The essay in *Extended reading* explains the cultural significance of food. By learning this unit, Ss will gain a deep understanding of cultural elements embodied in foods and they will treasure Chinese food culture and better present it to the world.

**Teaching suggestions**

**My first oyster**

### Text interpretation

本文讲述了作者第一次吃牡蛎的经历。在整个过程中，作者对牡蛎的感受有较大的转变：从最开始看到牡蛎时的恶心，到无法抵挡父亲多次劝说时的绝望，再到吃下牡蛎感受到大海气息时的惊喜。这次经历让作者明白一个道理：要敢于尝试新事物，哪怕一开始这件事看上去令人生畏。于作者而言，第一只牡蛎就是一节关于勇于尝试的宝贵的人生课。

文章探讨了"人们对食物的感受"这一生活话题，其反转的结尾带给读者无穷遐思。人们对食物的感觉往往受其外观和气味的影响。同时，每个人的个体经历也会影响其对食物的感受。那些家庭团聚的美好记忆、令人回味的童年轶事、充满爱意的暖心时刻往往都有美食的印迹。通过学习本文，学生能领悟到食物与生活的关系：食物不仅意味着感官体验，也带给我们生活体验，成为我们人生经历的一部分。我们在生活中体验食物，也在品尝食物中感受生活。

本文围绕儿子第一次尝试吃牡蛎时感受的转变这条线索展开，通过细节描写呈现父亲不断劝说下儿子态度的变化。通过课后理解练习 1 和 2，教师可以带领学生捕捉关键画面，领悟父亲的"坚持"和儿子的情感态度。作者擅用直接描写和间接描写，使得"吃牡蛎的感受"特别鲜活生动。教师可以带领学生阅读课文，了解直接描写和间接描写的特点，并结合课后练习分析两种描写方式的文本效果。

### Culture notes

**1 The first oyster**

The first oyster is presented by the writer as a "coming of age ceremony" (reaching the age of maturity, which in many cultures is marked with a special celebration). However, this isn't a general custom. Probably the writer wants to stress that eating oysters is considered a luxury, and eating the first oyster is a very grown-up activity. There is a ritual when eating oysters, though. Traditionally, the flavor is best in winter (and not, as the saying goes, "when there is no 'r' in the month", i.e., not to eat oysters between May and August, since those months have no letter "r"). To eat oysters you take the opened shell with one hand and "slurp" the oyster and savor the saltwater taste in your mouth, then drink the juice — with perhaps a touch of lemon juice or hot sauce — from the shell; and then eat a mouthful of bread between each variety of oyster to cleanse the mouth.

**2 Fish and chips**

Fish and chips is a piece of fish covered in batter (a mixture of flour, egg, and water) and fried in oil and served with chips. It is a very popular food in Britain and often bought from a restaurant to take home. Nowadays, fish and chip shops generally also serve other food, such as fried chicken or sausages (with chips), pizza, or shish kebab (烤羊肉串), and chips with curry. These last items reflect cultural influences on food, often as a result of immigration and international travel. These influences have greatly increased the range of dishes. Such changes are characteristic of most Western cultures, which in many respects are much more multicultural than they were, say, 40 years ago.

**世界各地的街头小吃**

1 "快餐"这个词通常让人想起那些包装好的便于忙碌的现代都市人食用的汉堡和薯条。

2 事实上，快餐并不是现代社会的产物。去世界上任何一个地方旅游，你都会看到街头小吃摊贩的身影，听见他们叫卖各种当地特色小吃的吆喝声。这些小吃通常都是用几样当地的基础食材加工而成，顾客等着现场制作。

3 商业化的快餐制作采用工业化的加工程序，用预先准备好的食材大量烹制，然后重新加热。你去到店里，点餐、付钱，几分钟后快餐就到你手中。这既节省时间，也让人信赖。当你在国际连锁快餐品牌用餐，你很清楚食物的味道在世界任何地方都是一样的。

4 而街头小吃都是顾客等着店主现场备菜并制作的，食物一般在街边小摊贩上售卖。由于食物是现场制作，顾客必须要等候，摊位前也往往是排成长队。通常情况下，你在哪里买的，就在哪里吃。

5 从中国的面条到土耳其的烤肉，世界各国都有街头小吃传统。在泰国，你能听见人们沿街叫卖配着花生酱吃的烤鸡。在印度，你可以品尝鲜热美味的炸蔬菜。在日本乘坐火车时，你甚至可以在上车前停下来买一份健康的含有蔬菜和米饭的鱼肉或其他肉类便当。即便你只品尝了这些小吃一次，那种美味也会在未来的岁月里停留在你的记忆里。在这些国家，街头小吃不仅仅是为了游客，也是当地人的一种生活方式，代表了他们的文化和传统。

6 街头小吃与商业快餐的区别在于它的地方特色。而且由于使用的是当地的食材，这些食物的风味总是独特且浓郁的。

**Extended reading**

**食物中的文化**

1 你是否有想过你每天吃的食物能告诉你你是来自哪儿的人？你是否有想过为什么世界各地的不同的人们吃不同的食物？你是否问过自己为什么某些食物或烹饪传统对你的文化如此重要？食物与文化之间的联系要比你所想的紧密得多。

2 我们吃着属于我们文化传统的食物长大。食物成为每个人的一部分。我们很多人将儿时的食物与温暖的感受和美好的回忆相联系，它能帮助家庭成员内部建立特殊的纽带。成年后每当遇到挫折和压力时，家里的食物往往成为我们想要寻求的安慰。小时候，我生病了，因为身体太虚弱，就没法吃饭。妈妈会煮些汤并端到我的床前。那种汤的香气和味道成为了我很熟悉的东西。现在，每当我感到很疲惫或者身处压力时，我就会想起妈妈为我煮的汤羹，我很怀念它。

3 食物是文化中很重要的一部分。传统烹饪从一代人传到下一代人，它也是文化身份的一种表达。移民不管去哪里都带着自己乡土的食物。当他们搬去新地方时，烹饪传统的食物成为保留自己文化的一种方式。

4 对移民来说，烹饪传统食物也是民族自豪感的象征，是应对乡愁的一种办法。很多人开了自己的餐馆，供应传统的菜肴。但是，这些食物的味道与故乡的食物不是一模一样的。比如，由于一些制作传统菜肴的原材料不易获得，因此做出来的味道可能无法与在故土时完全一样。

5 此外，当移民在其他国家售卖食物时，他们不仅仅只是卖给自己家乡的同胞，也卖给他们定居国家的居民。因此，他们必须改变原来的菜肴来迎合当地顾客的口味喜好。菜肴改变后会产生新的口味，不过还是会保留其文化精髓。保持不变的是每个国家或者社群的特定菜肴才能体现的其独特的历史、生活方式、价值观以及信念。

6 在中国，"和谐"在生活的各个方面都得到了高度重视。这也体现在中国菜肴中，几乎每一种口味（咸、辣、酸、甜和苦）都会被调理得平衡得当，各种味道相得益彰，成为美味的菜肴。历史上，中国人崇尚华美富丽的风格，这在建筑和服装上有所体现，也同样体现在食物上。他们认为食物不仅仅要有营养，还需要看起来有吸引力。他们付出很多努力来装饰菜肴，使它们看起来绚丽多彩。

7 美国人的饮食反映了他们国家的历史。欧洲对美洲的殖民使得一些欧洲食材和烹饪方法传入美国。二十世纪末期，从许多国家涌入的移民给美国带来了全世界丰富多元的食物。

8 如今，伴随着世界的全球化进程，接触来自不同文化的食物变得更加容易。我们应该通过食物来拥抱我们的文化，我们也应该通过尝试异域的食物来了解对方的文化。很重要的一点是要记住，每一道菜肴对于它所属的文化都具有特殊的位置，对于烹饪菜肴的人都具有特殊的意义。食物是了解文化的重要途径，一旦有机会，我们应该随时准备通过食物来了解文化。

# Unit 4 Handing it down

## Unit overview

This unit focuses on the importance of family life and family virtues. It aims to help Ss reflect on their personal experiences and gain an in-depth understanding of the personal, social, and cultural values of family education. The text in *Active reading* presents a typical family story illustrating how a father worked hard to help his son go to college and taught his son virtues like perseverance and devotion to the family. The text in *Further exploration* illustrates some Chinese family virtues with family stories of some great people in Chinese history. Then the audio explains the importance of telling family stories for the development of children. In *Project*, Ss are asked to tell a family story of their own behind an object and explain a family virtue of Chinese culture embodied in the story. The essay in *Extended reading* analyzes possible effects that people's families of origin may have on their mental health, social relationships, and self-perceptions. By learning this unit, Ss will develop a closer relationship with their family, better understand Chinese family virtues, and generate their own thoughts about family education.

**Teaching suggestions**
This is a prediction activity, so Ss should not look at the text beforehand. Remind Ss to make reasonable predictions on the basis of the sentences given since these are not questions of what is right or wrong, but of what is possible or reasonable.

### The pickle jar

#### Text interpretation

本文的背景是20世纪美国的一个小镇，作者回忆了父亲为自己积攒学费、谋求出路的故事，塑造了一个坚韧、勤劳、乐观且关爱家人的父亲形象。第一部分（第一至第四段）为作者的儿童时期。回忆了父亲用泡菜坛攒钱、带自己去银行存钱、鼓励自己上学谋求更好生活的场景。第二部分（第五至第六段）为作者大学毕业和结婚之后。体悟了父亲的行为对自己的影响，并和妻子分享了自己的感受和经历。第三部分（第七至第九段）为作者女儿出生后。作者发现父亲又开始为孙女攒钱，自己继承父亲的行为，也向泡菜坛内存钱。父亲注意到作者此举，二人四目相对，故事在此结束，留下丰富的品味空间。

文章通过叙述围绕一个泡菜坛子发生的一系列家庭故事，体现了父母对子女润物细无声的爱和教导。基于课文，教师可引导学生回忆并讲述个人的家庭故事，同时探讨父母对子女的影响、父母对子女的教育方式、父母与子女之间的情感交流、家庭教育传承的文化观念等话题，帮助学生树立正确的家庭观，挖掘课文的思政价值。

例1：第二至四段对父亲的行为、话语和表情的描写，体现了父亲对孩子的无私付出。
例2：第五段 "My dad was a man of few words, and never lectured me on the values of determination, perseverance, and faith. The pickle jar had taught me all these virtues far more eloquently than the most flowery of words could have done." 这一段话生动地说明了父母对子女的教育除了言传，更要注意身教。
例3：第九段一家三代之间的互动体现了关爱、节俭等品质在家庭代际之间的传承。

这是一篇典型的记叙文，按照作者的人生阶段进行叙述，时间顺序清晰。从写作手法上看，本文最突出的特征是以泡菜坛为象征物，围绕泡菜坛描述作者人生中的动人瞬间。教师可结合理解练习1引导学生关注不同阶段泡菜坛的作用，理解象征手法在文中的使用。故事叙事流畅，使用了大量的时间相关表达，教师可结合预热活动和理解练习1，引导学生关注这些表达，在翻译练习中夯实，并在产出任务中有效使用。文章对人物语言、行为和心理活动的描写非常细腻，教师可结合理解练习2引导学生分析细节描述背后的深意。

#### Language points

1   **When he got ready for bed, Dad would empty his pockets and toss his coins into the jar. (Para. 1)**
    **Notes:** Here "would" is used to describe an action that somebody often or always did in the past. The author uses this structure six times in the first three paragraphs to tell what his father kept doing during his childhood. This shows the determination of the father to save money for his son.

2   **Each and every time, as we drove to the bank, Dad would look at me hopefully. (Para. 2)**

（页底截断，接下页）

**Notes:** Here "each and every time" is put at the beginning of the sentence with a comma for emphasis. It shows that the father saw his son as the hope of the family and tried every effort to help him to go to college.

3   **Those coins are going to keep you out of the textile mill, son. (Para. 2)**
    **Meaning:** With the deposits, you would have a fund to go to college and get a better-paid job instead of working in a textile mill like me.

4   **This old mill town's not going to hold you back. (Para. 2)**
    **hold sb. / sth. back:** prevent sb. or sth. from making progress 阻碍某人或某事发展
    *Don't let fear hold you back from pursuing your dream.*

5   **"You'll get to college on pennies, nickels, dimes, and quarters," he said. (Para. 4)**
    **Meaning:** "With the coins in the jar, we will accumulate enough money to finance your college education," he said.

6   **I'll see to that. (Para. 4)**
    **Meaning:** I will deal with your college fund and you don't have to worry.
    **see to sb. / sth.:** deal with sth. or do sth. for sb. 照料；处理
    *You can take a rest. I'll see to the cleaning up.*
    **Notes:** The phrase "see to" is often used in the structure "see to it that ...," which means "make sure that ..." For example:
    *I'll see to it that he is told about the good news.*

7   **A lump rose in my throat as I stared at the spot beside the dresser where the jar had always stood. (Para. 5)**
    **Notes:** The expression "a lump in sb.'s throat" is an idiomatic one meaning "a feeling that somebody wants to cry." Literally, "lump" refers to "a small piece of something solid, without a particular shape." When people feel the urge to cry, there is an intense emotional reaction resulting in a sensation of tightness in the throat, so it feels like that there is a lump rising in the throat. That's why the idiomatic expression forms. The more common usage you can find in dictionaries is "a lump in one's throat," as in "There was a lump in my throat after I finished reading my grandfather's last letter."

8   **My dad was a man of few words, and never lectured me on the values of determination, perseverance, and faith. (Para. 5)**
    **lecture:** criticize sb. or tell them how you think they should behave, esp. when it is done in an annoying way 教训；训斥；说教

**家风：传统家庭美德**

1 家庭是社会的细胞。家庭美德蕴含了一个民族和文化的核心价值观。

2 家庭美德，即大家熟知的"家风"，指一个家庭所秉持的优秀的品质、习惯和道德准则。其中一种就是"孝"，即赡养、敬重父母的义务。这种品德代代相传。人们孝敬自己的父母，以这种方式教会孩子什么是"孝"。比如，鲁迅的母亲任劳任怨，侍奉自己年迈的公婆，她的行为深深影响了鲁迅。后来，母亲年迈时，鲁迅也以同样的方式侍奉母亲。据说，鲁迅甚至会收集爱情小说来使母亲欢欣。

3 家风也会以更正式的方式世代相传。在中国古代，一些大家族会把家风编撰成册，用作子孙后代的行为准则。最有名的作品之一当属《颜氏家训》。在这本书中，颜之推为子女留下了一整套人生哲学。他告诫子女走正途、远邪道，不可虚度一生。比如，他告诉子女，积财千万，无过读书；积财千万，不如薄技在身。这本写于 1400 多年前的书，现在仍是家庭教育的经典著作。

4 有些家风可以穿越数百年，成为整个民族秉信的准则。林则徐幼时，祖父在他心中种下了一颗信念的种子：报效国家就要振兴国家。19 世纪 30 年代，英国商人倾销鸦片，威胁中国的财政安全和人民的健康。林则徐下令销毁鸦片，并禁止鸦片贸易。与此同时，他又呼吁中国开眼看世界，号召国人学习他国最新的科学技术。到了 20 世纪，他的五世孙林墨卿传承家风，为国贡献，成为中华人民共和国的首批口译员和外交家，在改善中国同其他国家的关系上发挥了重要作用。

5 家庭是人生的第一所学校。无论时代如何变迁，家风和家庭教育永远都是社会进步和民族发展的基石。

# Unit 5 The admirable

## Unit overview

This unit focuses on the topic of heroes and aims to help Ss understand what a hero means and how they behave, gain an in-depth understanding of the differences between heroes in Western and Chinese cultures, and inspire Ss to be heroes. The text in *Active reading* presents the life story of a true food hero, Yuan Longping, who devoted his whole life to ending hunger in the world. The text in *Further exploration* illustrates heroes in Western literature. Then the audio describes two Chinese heroes – Yue Fei and Zhao Yiman, aiming to help Ss know more about Chinese heroic figures in China's traditional culture and revolutionary culture. In *Project*, Ss are asked to give a speech on the topic "A hero in my eyes." The essay in *Extended reading* explains "heroes" in the form of question and answer, addressing four questions about "heroes." The answers to these questions help Ss understand more about heroes, especially everyday heroism. By learning this unit, Ss can develop a deep understanding of heroes, tell the life story and describe the personal qualities of a hero in their eyes, and perform kind acts regularly.

## Scenario

The scenario in this unit can be analyzed from the following aspects:

**Setting:** An English public-speaking competition (Formal)

**Topic:** A hero in my eyes

**Identity:** Speaker – Student contestants
　　　　　Recipient – Teachers and students in the university

**Teaching suggestions**

T can help Ss brainstorm ideas about what a hero means, who their heroes are, and what their heroes have done. After Ss have identified their own heroes, T can ask them to try to describe the life experiences of their heroes and explain why they regard the person as a hero. Then T can ask them to share their ideas in groups.

After the tryout, T can ask Ss to reflect on their difficulties in four aspects, i.e., content (what difficulties they have in describing their heroes), language (what difficulties they have in expressions and sentence structures), structure (what difficulties they have in organizing a speech clearly and logically), and communication (what difficulties they have in making a speech inspiring), and write them down in the table in the Student's Book. T can also do a small survey to see which aspects are the most difficult points for most of the Ss in doing this project.

## Active reading

### Language points

1 **Medal of the Republic, a TV series commemorating the 100th anniversary of the Communist Party of China, tells the life stories of eight Chinese national heroes. (Para. 1)**

**commemorate:** *vt.* do sth. to show that you remember and respect sb. important or an important event in the past 庆祝；为……举行纪念活动

*Chinese National Day is celebrated on October 1 annually to commemorate the founding of the People's Republic of China.*

2 **He once dreamed of creating rice crops taller than sorghum, with each ear of rice as long as a broom and each grain as big as a peanut. (Para. 2)**

**Notes:** We use "as + adjective / adverb + as" to make comparisons when the things we are comparing are equal in some way. Here in this sentence, "ear of rice" and "grain" are compared with "broom" and "peanut", respectively. This shows how precious Yuan's dream was and how determinedly he worked to contribute to the food security of China and the world.

3 **The first breakthrough was made in 1970, when his team found a stretch of wild rice which made developing a hybrid rice theoretically possible. (Para. 3)**

**stretch:** *n.* [C] an area of land or water, esp. one that is long and narrow （尤指狭长的）一片地域，一片水域

*There is an immense stretch of forest along the road.*

4 **By the mid-1970s, he had determined that planting hybrid rice would increase rice yields by 20 to 30 percent. (Para. 3)**

**determine:** *vt.* find out the facts about sth. 查明；测定；确定

*The test is used to determine who is most suitable for the job.*

**Notes:** The word "by" in "by 20 to 30 percent" is used to show how much something has changed, or how much difference there is between things. For example:

*House prices went down by 15 percent.*

5 **Yuan had unlocked the secret to hybrid rice, and became a hero to the people of China. (Para. 4)**

**unlock the secret to / of sth.:** discover the most important facts about sth. 揭开某事的秘密

*The divers have unlocked some of the secrets of the sea.*

6 **It was estimated that the production increases brought about by hybrid rice have helped feed an extra 70 million Chinese people each year. (Para. 4)**

**estimate:** try to judge the value, size, speed, cost, etc. of sth., without calculating it exactly 估计；估算

*It is estimated that the project will cost about 8,000 dollars.*

**Notes:** Some common structures of the verb "estimate" under this meaning are "estimate that," "be estimated to be / have / cost, etc.," and "estimate how many / what, etc." For example:

*The total attendance at the concert was estimated to be over 50,000 people.*

*It is difficult to estimate how many people will attend the event, as registration is still ongoing.*

7 **In 2004, Yuan won the World Food Prize and was credited with helping "create a more abundant food supply and more stable world." (Para. 5)**

**credit sb. with doing sth.:** believe or say that sb. is responsible for doing sth., esp. sth. good 认为是……的功劳；把……归于

*That team of scientists are credited with inventing the industrial robot.*

**Notes:** The expression "credit A with B" can be replaced by "credit B to A." For example, the above sentence can be rewritten as "The invention of the industrial robot is credited to that team of scientists."

8 **Yuan kept chasing his two dreams into his 90s. (Para. 6)**

**Notes:** The word "into" in "into his 90s" is used for saying that something continues for a long time and ends part of the way through a later period (持续到；一直到). For example:

*Jack didn't get married until he was into his 50s.*

9 **Yuan devoted his whole life to researching hybrid rice, helping billions achieve food security. (Para. 6)**

**Meaning:** Yuan used most of his time and effort to conduct research on hybrid rice, which guaranteed that billions of people would have sufficient food.

10 **His lifelong dedication to reducing hunger earned him wide recognition at home and abroad, and made him a heroic figure. (Para. 6)**

**Notes:** The preposition after "dedication" should be "to," and a noun or gerund is followed.

11 **… "his mission to end hunger lives on." (Para. 7)**

**live on:** continue to live or exist 继续活着；继续存在

*Although Yuan Longping passed away, his spirit lives on.*

12 **This humble but great man is worth remembering. (Para. 7)**

**be worth doing:** used for saying that there is a good enough reason for doing sth., because it is important, enjoyable, useful, etc. 值得……的

1 乍看上去，过去的史诗和今日的报纸头条似乎没有什么相似之处。但经仔细品读后，你会发现二者大多都是关于鼓舞人心的英雄事迹。几千年来，英雄总是为人们津津乐道。他们拥有勇敢的灵魂，在危难之际无私帮助不幸之人。我们常常歌颂英雄，将他们的勇敢与决心视作特殊且珍贵的品质。然而，是否我们所有人实际上都具备英雄那般迎难而上与拯救生命的能力呢？

2 什么是英雄？习惯上，"英雄"一词常常使我们联想到在战场上舍生取义或在危险情境中实施救援的那些人。英雄主义被理解为冒险帮助他人却不求任何个人回报的行为。实际上，根据一位科学作家的说法，我们都拥有成为英雄的能力。这位作家依据最近关于人类英雄本性的科学研究，认为典型的英雄与花时间做志愿工作或安慰伤心的朋友的人并无区别。

3 我们生来便是英雄吗？答案是肯定的。关于人类慈善之举的研究证实了这一点。一位经济学家研究了人们向慈善事业捐款时的脑部活动，他重点关注大脑中一个叫作伏隔核的区域。大家知道这个区域是大脑处理奖赏和快乐的区域。这位经济学家发现，当研究参与者们做出捐赠的决定时，他们大脑中的伏隔核区变得异常活跃。也就是说，研究参与者们认为帮助他人是件好事。原因很容易理解：当我们决定无私地给予他人帮助时，我们会想象这样做会如何让他们受益。这种利他行为会使我们拥有目标感，而目标感正是获得生命满足感的重要来源。这种满足感也将激励我们在未来更积极地参与到慈善和正义事业中去。

4 经历过苦难的人更有可能成为英雄吗？一位研究者先前对经历过深重苦难的人展开了一项调查，他们当中包括曾遭遇暴力袭击和自然灾害的受害者。研究发现，这些曾经历苦难的人更富有同理心。例如，海啸的幸存者更有可能捐款帮助遭遇类似自然灾害的受难者。原因在于他们曾有过类似的遭遇，更容易与受难者产生共鸣。

5 如何着手将我们自己练就成为英雄呢？一位心理学家认为，成为英雄最好先从每天行好事做起。比方说做一些简单的小事，比如给饿肚子的人买些吃的，或者在工作中支持不堪重负的同事。尽管这些小小的善举并不足以称得上是英雄之举，但科学家们说，它们属于亲社会行为，而且还可以使我们理解他人的需要。日常行好事就是在生活中磨练我们的心智，如此一来，当出现需要我们拿出更多英雄气概的情况时，例如火场救人或者打击犯罪，我们才能更好地承受压力。日常行好事能够培养我们的英雄气概，在未来的某一天，这种英雄气概可能成为决定生死的英雄。

6 英雄主义不仅仅指为了拯救生命而将自己置于危险之中，它也可以是每天都在生活中做出小小的善举，而后者正是我们所有人都具备的能力。

### Culture notes

1 **COP26**

COP stands for the Conference of the Parties and is the annual UN Climate Change Conference. The COP is the supreme decision-making body of the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change (UNFCCC). The UNFCCC is an international treaty for international cooperation that entered into force in 1994 to combat climate change by limiting average global temperature increases and the resulting climate change, and coping with impacts that were, by then, inevitable. With 198 Parties, the UNFCCC has near-universal membership. Parties meet annually at the COP to monitor the progress made so far and work out the targets that need to be achieved in the future. Many of the most significant international climate commitments, agreements, and laws have emerged from these conferences over the years. COP26 is the 26th UN Climate Change Conference of the Parties held in Glasgow, the U.K. in November, 2021. The pre-COP26 was opened in Milan, Italy on September 30, 2021 to prepare for COP26 to be held.

2 **The Paris Agreement**

The Paris Agreement is a legally binding international treaty on climate change. It was adopted by 196 Parties at COP21 in Paris, on December 12, 2015 and entered into force on November 4, 2016. Its goal is to limit global warming to well below 2°C, ideally 1.5°C, compared to pre-industrial levels. To achieve this long-term temperature goal, countries aim to reach global peaking of greenhouse gas emissions as soon as possible to achieve a climate neutral world by the mid-21st century. The Paris Agreement is a landmark in the multilateral climate change process because, for the first time, a binding agreement brings all nations into a common cause to undertake ambitious efforts to combat climate change and adapt to its impacts.

A landfill site (废弃物填埋场) is an area used for disposing of waste materials, either by directly dumping them on the ground or filling up an unwanted hole in the ground. Arbor day, a holiday observed in many countries, is also known as the "Tree Planter's Holiday" in which individuals and groups are encouraged to plant trees. Long before other holidays and celebrations of nature, J. Sterling Morton proposed a tree-planting holiday to be called "Arbor Day" at a meeting of the Nebraska State Board of Agriculture on January 4, 1872. They set April 10, 1872 as the first observance of "Arbor Day." It is estimated that over one million trees were planted in Nebraska that day. Over the years, Arbor Day has grown into an international event. Every state in the U.S. and many countries celebrate Arbor Day on a date that is best suited to tree planting for their region. There are several ways to celebrate Arbor Day. Of course, the traditional way is to plant a tree, but you do not have to stop there. Arbor Day celebrations provide unique opportunities to educate people, get people together in a joint cause, and promote proper tree selection and care.

### Background information

**Common but differentiated responsibilities (CBDR) (共同但有区别的责任)**
The principle of "common but differentiated responsibilities (CBDR)," formalized at the 1992 United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change (UNFCCC) of Earth Summit in Rio de Janeiro, is ultimately pertaining to the matter of climate justice. Its basic meaning is first and foremost a "shared" moral responsibility between different groups of countries to address global climate change, nevertheless the proportions of such responsibility are differentiated. CBDR's underlying concepts are fairness and equity. It was the first international legal instrument to address climate change and the most comprehensive international attempt to address negative impacts to global environment. The CBDR principle acknowledges that all states have shared obligation to address environmental destruction but denies equal responsibility of all states with regard to environmental protection. In the Earth Summit, states acknowledged disparity of economic development between developed and developing countries. Industrialization proceeded in developed countries much earlier than it did in developing countries. CBDR is based on relationship between industrialization and climate change. The more industrialized a country is, the more likely that it has contributed to climate change. States came to an agreement that developed countries contributed more to environmental degradation and should have greater responsibility for climate change mitigation than developing countries should. The CBDR principle could therefore be said to be based on the polluter-pays principle where historical contribution to climate change and respective ability to combat climate change become measures of responsibility for environmental protection.

4 国家二氧化碳排放的历史也就是国家发展的历史。从 1850 年至 1910 年左右，全球二氧化碳排放由土地利用变化和森林砍伐主导。这一时期的排放大国中，有一些是幅员辽阔的国家，例如美国和俄罗斯。这些国家的人砍伐森林用于农业用地，将木材用作燃料。例如，在美国，一大批拓荒者从东到西遍布整个大陆，他们于所到之处开垦土地进行耕作。与此同时，一些欧洲国家正处于以煤炭为燃料的工业化进程中。它们在 1850 年之前已基本完成土地开垦。这些国家包括法国、德国，以及最主要的煤炭燃烧国——英国。从 1910 年起，煤炭和其他化石燃料的燃烧成为二氧化碳排放的又一主要原因。直到 1950 年之前，一半以上的历史二氧化碳排放量都来自于欧洲，这其中绝大部分来自英国。

5 这些国家如今也仍然是造成历史性变暖的重要国家。比如，美国在 1850 年至 2021 年间的累计二氧化碳排放总量位居世界第一，这始于煤炭的广泛使用，继于汽车时代的到来。到 2021 年底，美国的二氧化碳排放量占 1850 年以来全球总量的 20.3%，这是迄今为止最大的份额，并且与约 0.2℃ 的全球变暖有关。

6 另一个常见的论点是，发达国家虽然实现了国内减排，但仍依赖海外进口的高碳产品。例如，美国政客坚称美国有最清洁的空气和水资源。目前美国的空气和水质水平是在 20 世纪 70 年代末至 80 年代初确立的，那时美国开始向发展中国家离岸外包，主要目的地是北美（如墨西哥）和亚洲（如中国和印度）。美国将制造业转移至海外的同时，也将污染转移至了海外。

7 为处理这一问题，第 21 届联合国气候变化大会于 2015 年 12 月 12 日通过了《巴黎协定》。据此协定，因他国而产生二氧化碳的国家不应独自承担其高排放相关的成本与负担，应允许这些国家索要回由减少污染带来的费用。

8 事实上，发达国家和发展中国家在应对气候变化方面承担的责任一直是国际社会高度关注的话题。早在 1994 年，《联合国气候变化框架公约》就确立了"共同但有区别的责任"这一原则。发达国家作为历史及当前的温室气体排放大国，应带头减排，并向发展中国家提供财政和技术支持。例如，它们应该尽快兑现在 2009 年第 15 届联合国气候变化大会上作出的承诺，即直至 2020 年，每年为发展中国家的气候行动提供 1,000 亿美元。同时，发展中国家应以减排温室气体为奋斗目标，调整发展计划，转向更绿色的经济增长之路。

9 地球是我们的共同家园，保护地球是全人类的共同责任。只有全世界共同努力，才能遏制地球变暖的步伐，并有望在可预见的未来终止全球变暖。
