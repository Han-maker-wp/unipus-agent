# Unit 1 Money smarts

## Unit overview

This unit discusses the development of personal financial literacy, aiming to help Ss learn to be financially responsible for their life. The text in *Active reading* narrates a girl's experience in her childhood about how she gradually understands the value of money and learns to look after herself financially. After this touching and enlightening story, the text in *Further exploration* provides sensible and practical advice for international students about how to study and live in China on a budget. Furthermore, the audio shares the basic steps of money management to help the listener achieve personal and financial goals. In *Project*, Ss are asked to give international students advice on money management. The excerpt from a bestseller *Tuesdays with Morrie* in *Extended reading* guides us in the exploration of a genuinely satisfying life beyond money. By learning this unit, Ss are expected to acquire essential skills in money management, enhance money wisdom, and thus improve their life satisfaction in the long run.

**Scenario**
学生作为其所在大学的国际学生服务中心的志愿者，参与编写国际学生手册中的财务单元，向学校的国际学生提出理财建议，让他们更好地在中国生活。

**Active reading**
(Look after yourself)
阅读个人财商养成的故事，初步获取理财知识，了解理财意识对人生的意义。

**Further exploration – Text**
(Enjoy studying in China on a budget)
阅读理性建议，了解留学生在中国生活和学习的省钱小诀窍，进一步提升理财意识。

**Further exploration – Audio**
(Steps to better money management)
聆听短文，学习理财知识，助力实现理财规划和人生目标。

**Project**
结合本章阅读和音频中语言和内容要点，总结自己生活中做预算、省钱、攒钱经验，为国际学生提供实用的理财建议，提升其中国的学习和生活体验。

**Extended reading**
(Tuesdays with Morrie)
阅读畅销书《相约星期二》的节选，探索物质财富以外的人生意义，促进个人成长。

**Self-reflection**
回顾整个单元的学习，反思在内容、语言、结构、交际和行为上的收获。

结合个人生活经历，总结理财实践经验，分享实用理财技巧，提倡勤俭节约的传统美德，培养理财规划的能力，提升财商，树立积极的金钱观、人生观。

## Scenario

The scenario in this unit can be analyzed from the following aspects:

**Setting:** The International Students Service Center at your university

**Topic:** Money management

**Identity:** Writer – Volunteer student working at the center

Recipient – International students at the university

**Purpose:** To provide advice on how to manage money in China

**Teaching suggestions**

T can help Ss generate ideas by breaking the topic of managing money into different categories, such as, “budgeting,” “reducing spending,” and “building savings.”

By brainstorming and filling into the categories with the ideas that pop into their mind, Ss will gradually get a general idea regarding what information they are supposed to collect, what they should notice in language use and how to use language effectively in doing the project.

After the tryout, T can ask Ss to reflect on their difficulties in four aspects: content, language, structure, and communication, and write them down in the table in the Student's Book. T can also do a small survey to identify the most challenging aspects of the project for the majority of Ss.

## Active reading

The task in *Scenario* asks Ss to provide advice on money management for international students. The text is an initiation story about a girl, illustrating the significance of developing financial literacy, and giving Ss a preliminary understanding of how to make a budget. It can also inspire Ss to reflect on their money management practices, and thus understand the theme of the unit, e.g. how to become wiser financially and how to better plan for their future. Ss can learn basic techniques for crafting a well-told story that attracts the reader's attention and conveys a meaningful message.

### Look after yourself

### Text interpretation

本文的背景是上世纪二战后，当时大量移民从加勒比海地区迁移到北美大陆和欧洲，作者的父亲也是在这一时期从圣卢西亚的贫寒之家到英国谋求出路。在课文的第一部分（第一至第六段），作者回忆了自己的童年家境，虽然家庭氛围快乐、温馨，却不得不在经济上精打细算。同时作者讲述了父亲如何一路奋斗，到英国成家立业。他努力工作，却仍旧需要省吃俭用来更好地照顾家人。在塑造了一个坚韧、勤劳、节俭的父亲形象的同时，作者围绕自己的生日心愿，叙述了童年难忘的经历。在课文的第二部分（第七至第十八段），作者讲述节省的父亲既没有一口回绝女儿的请求，也没有轻易满足女儿的愿望，而是鼓励女儿调查养宠物狗的花费，并规划如何才能负担这笔费用。在一番调查、计划后，女儿实现了自己的生日愿望，但同时有了更重要的收获。第三部分（第十九至第二十段），女儿在知命之年，回忆这段往事，心中充满了对父亲的感激，感谢他培养了让自己受益终身的理财观。

文章通过叙述为实现一个生日心愿而发生的一系列家庭故事，体现了父母对子女在金钱方面润物细无声的教导。文章提倡的勤俭持家、精打细算和中华民族“勤俭节约”的传统美德相吻合。教师可引导学生回忆并讲述个人的家庭故事，回顾自己的家庭理财传统，探讨在自己的家庭，父母是如何在金钱、理财等方面和自己进行讨论，帮助自己树立理财意识，培养正确的金钱观的。

这是一篇典型的记叙文，时间顺序清晰。文章从作者的童年开始，穿插对父亲金钱观形成的背景介绍，围绕作者童年一个生日心愿的实现，用一个小故事来传达培养孩子理财意识的重要意义。从写作手法上看，本文最突出的特征是通过细节性的描述和生动的故事情节，来确立、传达某种积极向上的价值观。教师可以结合预读活动、理解练习和写作策略引导学生注意这种写作手法，并在 *Sharing your ideas* 部分尝试使用这种策略。同时，课文中出现了大量和理财有关的词汇表达，教师可以引导学生在语言练习中夯实，并在产出任务中有效使用。

### Language points

1  Look after yourself (Title)
**Notes:** “Look after oneself” can mean “take care of one's health, finances, life, or any aspects to make sure that things happen to one's advantage.”

2  While we weren't wealthy by any means, it was an exceedingly happy and harmonious childhood. (Para. 1)
not by any means / by no means: not at all 绝不；一点儿都不
She is not an inexperienced driver by any means.

3  Now, happy kids don't know the meaning of sorrow. (Para. 2)
know / not know the meaning of sth.: (infml.) used for emphasizing how much or little you understand sth. 知道或不知道某事的意思（用于强调对某事物了解的多寡）
He's a terrible friend because he doesn't know the meaning of loyalty.

4  … I had a somewhat unusual birthday present in mind … (Para. 2)
have sth. / sb. in mind (for sth.): have an idea about what or who you want for a particular purpose 心里已有某事物或某人（作为……的目标选项）
They've already had her in mind for this position.

5  … his forehead creased in concentration as he went through all sorts of papers and wrote out cheques to pay the household bills. (Para. 3)
**Meaning:** As he carefully looked through different papers and filled out cheques to pay for the household bills, he concentrated deeply, causing his forehead to crease.
go through sth.: read or discuss sth. in order to make sure it is correct 审阅……；讨论某事
I always start the day by going through my email.
write out: write information on a check or a form 填写（支票或表格）
She sat at the table and began to write out a check to pay for her monthly rent.

6  … he was spotted by a film producer … (Para. 4)
**Notes:** While “spot” can simply mean “notice sth. or sb.,” it can also mean “notice a talent that can be developed.” This usage gives rise to the word “talent-spotter” (sb. whose job is to look for new performers or sports players).”

7  … he decided to come to England and try his luck as an actor here. (Para. 5)
try / chance your luck: do sth. because you hope you will be successful, even though you know you may not be 碰碰运气
Jack is desperate for a job and wants to try his luck at the port.

8  Mixed marriages were unusual and frowned on at the time … (Para. 6)
frown on / upon sth. / sb.: disapprove of sth. or sb., esp. sb.'s behavior 不赞成，反对（尤指某人的行为）
Personal phone calls are frowned on at work.

9  “Hello, precious,” he replied. (Para. 10)
**Notes:** “Precious (宝贝儿)” can be used to address sb. you love, esp. a baby or small child.

10  … because we had to keep some savings “for a rainy day,” … (Para. 12)
for a rainy day: if you save sth. or put sth. away for a rainy day, you save it for a time in the future when you may need it 以备不时之需；未雨绸缪
It makes good sense to save some money for a rainy day.

11  So I started to work out all the costs over the dog's life. (Para. 13)
work out: calculate sth. 计算；算出
It is proving hard to work out the value of bankrupt companies' assets.
**Notes:** This text uses a variety of words and expressions to indicate “finding out the cost of sth.,” such as “work out all the costs over … (Para. 13),” “reckoned how much it would cost to … (Para. 13),” “calculated the food costs … (Para. 13),” “find out what it would cost to … (Para. 14),” and “make an informed prediction about the vet's fees (Para. 16).”

12  I ploughed on and found out what it would cost to … (Para. 14)
plough on: continue doing sth. that is difficult or boring 坚持做，继续进行（艰难或乏味的事）
No one was listening to her, but she ploughed on regardless.

13  But I decided to persevere. (Para. 15)
persevere: vi. continue trying to do sth. in a very determined way in spite of difficulties – use this to show approval 锲而不舍，坚持不懈（含褒义）
The education director is persevering in his attempt to obtain additional funding for the school.

14  … containing information about a pet insurance scheme. The regular premiums were really quite small … (Para. 16)
premium: n. [C] the cost of insurance, esp. the amount that you pay each year （尤指每年缴付的）保险费
The premium for his car insurance was high due to his previous accidents.
**Notes:** The right pet insurance (宠物医疗保险) policy can protect pet owners against a portion of the costs associated with vet bills resulting from injuries caused by accidents, such as dog attacks and car accidents. This is a way to assist pet owners in covering the expenses of expensive pet medical treatments.

## Further exploration

### Enjoy studying in China on a budget (Text)

### Culture notes

**The train system in China**

China's train system is one of the world's most advanced and efficient systems of transportation. Currently, China's railway network has surpassed 150,000 kilometers in total operating mileage, with the high-speed rail accounting for over 40,000 kilometers, ranking as the world's largest network.

### Language points

1  Enjoy studying in China **on a budget** (Title)
on a budget: spending only as much money as you have 在预算内；按照预算
She managed to plan a memorable vacation on a budget by finding affordable accommodations and exploring free or low-cost activities.
**Notes:** In this text, the author uses different words and expressions to describe the frugal lifestyle. For example, “... on a budget” in the title, “budget-friendly,” and “economical” all describe the kind of prudent and frugal lifestyle.

2  Try **squeezing** two or three of them **into** your routine and watch your change jar fill up. (Para. 1)
**Meaning:** If you try to make some minor lifestyle changes, you will gradually save up money.
squeeze sth. into / in sth.: manage to do sth. although you are very busy 挤出时间做（某事）
Even on business trips, he manages to squeeze in a few rounds of golf.
**Notes:** Here the author uses the phrase “squeeze … into …” to emphasize these efforts are just very small changes in students' everyday life. “Change jar” literally means the container people use to put into coins and here refers to their savings.

3  Many vendors in China today prefer to be in paid with apps, which makes it easier to **keep track of** your spending on your smartphone. (Para. 2)
keep track of sth. / sb.: pay attention to sth. or sb., so that you know where they are or what is happening to them 了解某事的动态 / 某人的情况
I watch the news to keep track of current events.

4  This **makes for** a great opportunity to … (Para. 3)
make for: help to make sth. possible 促成
Eating a balanced diet and getting enough sleep can make for improved overall health and well-being.

5  Taxis can be convenient but, although not **prohibitive**, they are **pricier** than some of the public transportation that is available in large cities all over China. (Para. 4)
prohibitive: a. prohibitive costs are so high that they prevent people from buying or doing sth. （价格）高得负担不起的，贵得使人望而却步的
The cost of tuition at that Ivy League institution is prohibitive for most students, making it difficult for them to attend.
pricey: a. (pricier, priciest) (infml.) expensive 昂贵的；高价的

## Steps to better money management (Audio)

### Scripts

1  There's a misconception that to be good with money, you need a lot of it. Not true! What you need to be good with money is everyday management. Whether you're planning for yourself or for your whole family, there are three basic steps you can take to make the most of your money. One, create a budget.

Two, set savings goals. And three, tackle your debts. When put into practice, these steps can have a big impact not only on your monthly budget, but on your overall financial future.

2  One of the first steps to better money management is to create a budget and stick to it. This might sound simple, but you'd be surprised how few people actually do it. You can think of your budget as your guide to reaching your financial and personal goals. If you have trouble covering all your expenses each month, a budget can help you avoid overspending. That's because your budget can help you see and understand exactly where your money is going and whether or not your spending is in line with your personal goals.

3  The next step is to set savings goals. With your budget in place, building your savings will be that much easier, because you'll know how much extra money you have each month to allot to your goals. One of the best savings goals to start with is an emergency fund. Building up an emergency fund to help cover unexpected expenses, like a sudden medical bill, major home or car repair, or even a job loss, can help you avoid going into debt when life throws you a curveball, which it will. Instead of borrowing money to cover these emergencies, you'll already have the money saved up and this could end up saving you a lot more money in the long run. Start by building up three months' worth of expenses as a goal. Once you've established an emergency fund and are living within your budget, you can then figure out some long-term savings goals. Whether you decide to plan for your retirement, or save for a home improvement, college or even a well-deserved vacation, you'll be better able to set aside some money and have a timeline for reaching your goals.

4  The third step is to tackle any debts you have. First, as you're working to pay them down, you'll probably want to stop adding to the debts you already have. The less debt you have, the easier it'll be to get out from under it. It could also be helpful to know what your debt is costing you each month. Once you know how much your debt costs, you can create a plan that helps you reduce it and eventually pay it off. The sooner you get started, the more money you can save. It's worth noting that managing your debt and your savings go hand in hand. For instance, if you have a debt with a very high-interest rate, it may make sense to focus on paying it down at the same time or even before you build your entire emergency fund. As you make a plan to tackle your debt, setting target goals can help you stay on track as you actually see and feel the progress you're making.

5  These three steps are the basic components of money management, and it's easy to see how they can work together. By keeping a budget, you'll know what you have available to accomplish your savings goals and tackle your debts. Having an emergency fund can help you avoid adding any new debt, and occasionally checking in and reviewing your budget from time to time can help you set long-term savings goals, like a down payment on a home, as your priorities and circumstances change. Now that you have an understanding of the basics, why not take the next step? Being smarter with your money, learning new tips and techniques can help you today and down the road.

## Tuesdays with Morrie

### Background information

The writer Mitch Albom (1958– ) is an internationally renowned and bestselling author and sports journalist. In 1995, after seeing his former sociology professor Morrie Schwartz appear on television, Mitch phoned Schwartz and was prompted to visit him. He started visiting the old man of 79 who had been diagnosed with ALS and confined to his wheelchair with his health fading week by week.

After the professor died, Albom wrote the book *Tuesdays with Morrie* about the visits and his discussions with him. The book was published in 1997 and was a great success, heading the non-fiction bestseller list in the U.S. for a long time. The basic set-up: A wise old man, who is close to death and rich with his life experience, passes on his wisdom to his disciples. This is a familiar one from fiction and non-fiction. The professor's talks are basically sermons, encouraging people to seek meaning in life from goodness and not material possessions. It seems to be true of all prosperous societies that people worry about where their wealth is leading them, and fear materialism will alienate them from spiritual values and make their society uncaring, selfish, and money-mad.

In this excerpt from the book, the professor conveys these ideas clearly. The style is a very simple one.

# Unit 2 A good read

## Unit overview

This unit centers around the theme of reading, aiming to nurture Ss' passion for books while enhancing their ability to share these books with others. The text in *Active reading* explains, through quotes and evidence, the power of reading from three perspectives: how it defines our lives, how it helps us learn about different people or cultures, and what pleasure and satisfaction it may bring. Then the text and the video in *Further exploration* further illustrate the significance of reading through two novels, *The old man and the sea* and *Harry Potter*, which tell fascinating stories about how to fight against nature and discrimination respectively. They also serve as good examples of how to summarize and evaluate a book. The text in *Extended reading* introduces a slow-reading movement, emphasizing the importance of concentration during reading. By learning this unit, Ss are expected to be able to recommend a book that has a profound impact on them and to realize the benefits that reading has brought to their lives.

### Text interpretation

本文以论说形式强调了阅读的意义。文章以《爱丽丝梦游仙境》中主人公的经历和感受为切入点，指出阅读让读者进入全新世界，在风格各异、精彩纷呈的书籍中了解不同的人群和文化。作者接着进一步阐释阅读如何影响人们的生活方式，界定人生，并引发书籍对自己产生影响的思考。随后，作者介绍了“全垒打书籍”这一概念，通过棒球的生动比喻说明书籍能给读者带来的愉悦感和满足感。文章最后点题，强调阅读之所以“危险”，是因为它能改变人生。

文章从三个层次说明了阅读对人生的积极影响。教师可在此基础上组织学生根据切身体会对这三个层次进行深入讨论，并延申至阅读的其他益处，引导学生爱上阅读，学会阅读，阅读好书，从书籍中拓展视野，汲取力量，树立正确的价值观。

文章采用大量关于书籍的例证和名人名言的引用，增强了观点的说服力和趣味性。教师可结合理解练习引导学生梳理文章主要论点和论据，了解相应书籍内容，赏析引言的含义及其对观点的支撑作用。文章的另一大特色是排比与重复的运用，突显了作者表达的思想。教师可结合写作技巧板块讲授该修辞手法的用法和功能。此外，文中还有不少有关阅读意义的词块，有利于学生的产出任务。教师可结合语言练习，让学生学会活学活用。

### Culture notes

1 **Lewis Carroll**

Lewis Carroll (1832–1898) is the pen name of Charles Dodgson. He was a priest, a mathematician who taught at Oxford University, a photographer, humorist and writer of children's literature. *Alice's Adventures in Wonderland* (1865) was immediately successful, a masterpiece that revolutionized children's literature, giving coherence and logic through wit and humor to unlikely or impossible episodes in which imaginary creatures embody recognizable human characteristics. He is also known for *Through the Looking-Glass* (1871) and poems, such as *The Hunting of the Snark* (1876).

2 **William Cowper**

William Cowper (1731–1800) was a notable English poet, writer of hymns and letter writer. He wrote gentle, pious, direct poems about everyday rural life and scenes of the countryside. He was one of the forerunners of the Romantic Movement, and Coleridge called Cowper "the best modern poet." He translated Homer's Greek epics *The Odyssey* and *The Iliad* into English.

3 **John Steinbeck**

John Steinbeck (1902–1968) was a U.S. novelist who was awarded the Nobel Prize for Literature in 1962. *The Grapes of Wrath* (1939) is a well-known, long tragic novel about an American family of farmers who are driven off their land in Oklahoma by soil erosion in the famous Dust Bowl area. The book won the Pulitzer Prize and was made into a film in 1940. Other well-known novels include *Of Mice and Men* (1937), *Cannery Row* (1945), *The Pearl* (1947), *East of Eden* (1952) and an account of a personal rediscovery of America, *Travels with Charley: In Search of America* (1962).

4 **Graham Greene**

Graham Greene (1904–1991) was a British novelist, short-story writer, playwright, and journalist. He wrote a number of thrillers (he called them "entertainments") that dramatize an ambiguous moral dilemma, often revealing guilt, treachery, failure, and a theme of pursuit. Greene was also a film critic and all of the following novels have been made into films: *Brighton Rock* (1938), *The Power and the Glory* (1940), *The Heart of the Matter* (1948), *The Third Man* (1949), *The Quiet American* (1956), and *Our Man in Havana* (1958).

5 **Audrey Niffenegger**

Audrey Niffenegger (1963– ) is a U.S. college professor who teaches writing to visual artists and shows students how to make books by hand. Her first novel, *The Time Traveler's Wife* (2003) – made into a film in 2009 – is a science fiction and romance bestseller about a man who travels uncontrollably in time to his past and future, visiting his wife in her childhood, youth, and old age. His wife needs to cope with his absences and dangerous experiences while he travels. The story is a metaphor for distance and miscommunication in relationships.

6 **Paul Torday**

Paul Torday (1946–2013) was a British writer. *Salmon Fishing in the Yemen* (2007) was his first novel. It is a political satire and comedy about a dull civil servant who becomes involved in a plan to populate the desert with Scottish salmon. Politicians manage the media to spin this as a plan they support in order to divert attention from problems in the Middle East. There are themes of cynicism and belief and East-West culture clashes.

7 **E. M. Forster**

E. M. Forster (1879–1970) was a British novelist and writer of short stories and essays. He lived at different periods in Italy, Egypt and India and taught at Cambridge University. His best-known novels include *A Room with a View* (1908), *Howards End* (1910), *A Passage to India* (1924) which have all been made into films. His writing about reading and writing includes a book of lectures, *Aspects of the Novel* (1927).

8 **William Blake**

William Blake (1757–1827) was a British poet, artist and mystic, who was acknowledged as one of the first leading figures of the Romantic Movement. He made many engravings to illustrate the work of such writers as Virgil, Dante, and Chaucer, as well as his own poems. He stressed that imagination was more important than rationalism and the materialism of the 18th century and criticized the effects of the Industrial Revolution in England, but his work was largely disregarded by his peers. He is best known for his poetry in *Songs of Innocence* (1789) and *Songs of Experience* (1794). His belief in the oneness of all created things is shown in his much-quoted verse, "To see the world in a grain of sand, / And a heaven in a wild flower, / Hold infinity in the palm of your hand, / And eternity in an hour."

### Language points

1 **We become observers of life from the point of view of a person older than ourselves, or through the eyes of a child. (Para. 1)**

**Notes:** Both "from the point of view of" and "through the eyes of" are phrases that express a shift in perspective or viewpoint. For example:

*Exploring the history museum, we learned about ancient civilizations from the point of view of the archeologists who unearthed their artifacts.*

*Reading the novel allowed us to delve into the struggles of the protagonist through the eyes of a character with a unique and poignant perspective.*

2 **... but never less than liberating from the real world we come from. (Para. 1)**

**Meaning:** Whatever experience we get in reading, it can always liberate us from the real world for some time.

3 **Variety's the very spice of life, / That gives it all its flavor. (Para. 2)**

**Meaning:** Spices are made from plants and added to food to give it its particular flavor or taste. This quote from Cowper's poem means that variety gives life extra value and allows us to appreciate life in particular ways.

4 **Normally, we don't know the extent of these differences, yet sometimes when something bizarre happens to make us notice, variety and difference appear more as a threat than an opportunity. (Para. 2)**

**Meaning:** We usually don't know how big these differences are. But when something strange happens, we would notice these differences and find them frightening instead of helpful.

5 **To interact with other people's lives in the peace and quiet of our homes is a privilege which only reading fiction can afford us. (Para. 3)**

**The old man and the sea (Text)**

### Background information

*The Old Man and the Sea* is a short, heroic novel by Ernest Hemingway. It was published in 1952, marking the author's last major work of fiction.

The story centers on an aging fisherman who engages in an epic battle to catch a giant marlin. Hemingway was fascinated with ideas of men proving their worth by facing and overcoming the challenges of nature. The novel was an immediate success and came to be regarded as one of Hemingway's finest works. The Nobel Prize in Literature 1954 was awarded to Hemingway "for his mastery of the art of narrative, most recently demonstrated in *The Old Man and the Sea*, and for the influence that he has exerted on contemporary style."

### Language points

1 **It is considered to be Hemingway's crowning achievement as a literary giant. (Para. 1)**

**Notes:** This sentence makes a comment on both Hemingway and his work, *The Old Man and the Sea*. Both the words "crowning" and "giant" are used figuratively and vividly to depict the extraordinary qualities possessed by the novel and the author respectively.

2 **On one level, it is the tale of a man and a fish; on another, it is a story of the culture of manliness, courage, and bravery in the face of hardship and exhibits the human thirst for a meaningful life; then it is a history of what life was like when individuals were more the central actors on the human stage and not groups or organizations. (Para. 1)**

**Notes:** This sentence introduces the main idea and the theme of *The Old Man and the Sea* from three different perspectives. It uses the writing technique of parallelism and repetition. "On one level," "on another" and "then" is a parallel structure; "the tale of," "a story of," and "a history of" is another. The phrase "it is" is repeated three times. Both parallelism and repetition are used to emphasize the three points made in the novel.

**Further exploration**

**老人与海**

1 欧内斯特·海明威的《老人与海》是一个鼓舞人心的故事。它被认为是文学巨匠海明威的最高成就。一方面，它是关于一个人和一条鱼的故事。另一方面，它是关于困境中展现刚毅、勇气和胆识的故事，展示了人类对有意味的生活的渴望。此外，这也是一部反映当时生活的历史，那时站在人类舞台中心的演员主要是个人，而非团体或组织。

2 故事的最基本情节是这样的：在连续84天都没有钓到一条像样的鱼后，年迈的渔民圣地亚哥又乘小渔船出海了。他走得很远，钓到了一条18英尺长的巨型枪鱼。接下来战斗开始了，大鱼把小船和圣地亚哥拖到了很远的海上。圣地亚哥和大鱼搏斗了两天，用尽了所有心思，花光了所有力气。他终于在第三天捕获大鱼，把它绑到小船上。但在返程时，他却成为鲨鱼的猎物，鲨鱼分食了大鱼。当他到家时，大鱼只剩下了空骨架。但他依然沉沉地、平静地睡着了，梦到了年轻时的自己。“人可以被毁灭，但不能被打败，”海明威这样描述圣地亚哥。他歌颂了这位老人的勇气，以及独立、激烈、勇敢和充满力量的人性品质。

3 故事的叙述极为简洁，但这种简单也是它的力量所在。它在揭示人类内在力量的真谛方面极具洞察力。

4 欧内斯特·海明威的世界不是我的世界。今天的文化不再需要海明威所颂扬的和圣地亚哥所体现的那种激进的个人。然而，海明威有很强的叙述能力，让我不由自主地支持圣地亚哥，钦佩他，同情他，并为他输给比他更强大的力量而感到痛心。

5 还有一个副线故事。对于这位非凡、独立的个体而言，孤独并非他的自愿选择。他与一个小男孩建立了友谊，这个男孩五岁时就开始和他一起打渔。现在男孩已经换到了另一艘船上，但他渴望和圣地亚哥一起工作。与那条大鱼搏斗时，圣地亚哥一遍又一遍地祈求：“我希望那个男孩也在这里。”

6 与今天可能会读到这本小说的很多读者一样，我生活在城市，生活轻松舒适，远离与自然的严酷对抗。但海明威迫使我记得并承认个体的存在，为最基本的生存而奋斗，为生存而与自然抗衡。但更重要的是，他让人们认识到个人的重要性，以及勇气、技能、艺术和耐力的壮丽之处。

**Extended reading**

**慢读的艺术**

在互联网上无休止地浏览短文会让我们变得更愚蠢吗？虽然尚未有证据充分证实，但越来越多的专家持肯定态度。他们还提到，是时候慢下来了……

1 如果您正在读这篇文章的纸质版，您也许只能读完一半。如果您正在网上读这篇文章，您可能连五分之一都读不完。至少，这是两个研究项目，即波因特新闻研究院的“眼动跟踪”调查和雅各布·尼尔森分析的结论。两项研究都表明，现在很多人已经不再能精力集中地读完一篇文章。

2 而问题还不止于此：学者报告称，我们也不能专心读书了。巴斯巴大学的一位讲师透露，他不得不缩短学生的阅读清单，而牛津大学的一位历史学家则写道，他的年轻同事只用搜索引擎分析资料，他们不会阅读完整的资料，他对此感到很困惑。

3 那么，我们是不是变得越来越愚蠢了？这就是这个问题的症结所在吗？在某种程度上是这样的。据技术专家尼古拉斯·卡尔的著作《浅薄》所述，我们过度活跃的网络习惯正在破坏我们处理和理解冗长文本信息所需的智力。我们的阅读方式机械又肤浅，让大脑无法在各种想法之间搭建逻辑联系。无休止的新闻推送分散了我们的注意力，让我们快速从一篇文章跳到另一篇文章，却不能完全理解任何一篇文章的内容。最新电子邮件的提示声经常打断我们的阅读，我们现在更习惯吸收社交媒体上蹦出的短语，不习惯阅读长文本。

4 这一切都意味着，因为互联网，我们变得善于收集各种各样的事实碎片，却逐渐忘记了如何坐下来反思，如何把所有事实联系起来。因此，正如卡尔所写的，“我们逐渐无法在这两种截然不同的思维状态之间取得平衡。在精神上，我们处于永久的运动状态。”

5 还在读这篇文章？那么你可能属于极少数。你们这个极少数群体极易辨认，而且人数还在缩减。但这不重要：文学革命即将来临。我们先有慢饮食，然后有慢旅游。现在，加入其中的还有各行各业的学者和知识分子发起的慢阅读运动。他们希望我们放慢阅读和重新阅读的速度。他们要求我们偶尔关闭电脑，重新发现亲身接触纸质文本的乐趣，重获综合处理文本的能力。他们认为，如果你想要深入体验一本书，将其内化，把作者的想法与自己的想法融合，使它成为一种更个人化的体验，你就必须慢速阅读。

6 但是，第一位普及“慢读”这一术语的现代作家兰斯洛特·R.弗莱彻说，关于这个术语可能存在争议。他认为，慢读与其说是释放读者的创造力，不如说是发掘作者的意图。他希望将重点重新转移到作者的意图上，而不是读者对文本的解读上。

7 虽然最初这个术语是作为学术工具使用的，但慢读已经成为一个更广泛的概念。就像慢食一样，慢读如今本质上是一个本土主义的理念，可以帮助读者与社区建立联系。它被视为重视搭建人与思想之间联系的社区活动。通过借阅朋友的书籍，或者给孩子们读长篇故事，伴他们入睡，阅读让我们的关系得以延续。与此同时，尽管这一运动始于学术界，但英语教授、慢读运动博客作者特蕾西·西利强烈认为，慢读不应只限于学术界。细致的慢读和高度注意力是我们所有人面临的挑战。

8 在与一些学生交谈后，西利发现大多数人集中注意力阅读一段文字的时间超过30秒或一分钟。他们受到新技术的影响，越来越不会慢读了。但与其他一些教授不同的是，西利并不想减少她给学生布置的阅读量。“挑战我的学生是我的责任，”西利说。“我不想就此放弃。”

9 《如何读懂经典》的作者亨利·希金斯认为，部分原因在于互联网。它使我们习惯于新的阅读、观察和消费方式，缩短我们的专注时间，并瓦解我们持续集中注意力的能力。这种情况下，你不适合阅读《战争与和平》这样的书。他还认为，互联网的真正问题可能在于它慢慢侵蚀了人们的自我意识，侵蚀了人们独自享受快乐的能力，这种快乐自纸质书普及以来一直是读者的常规体验。

10 那么，我们该怎么办呢？与我交谈过的所有慢读的读者都意识到，完全抵制网络过于激进，甚至不切实际，但许多人认为暂时远离技术是可行的。例如，特蕾西·西利的学生提倡每周关一天电脑。但是，考虑到我们大多数人的生活节奏，我们真的有关电脑的时间吗？西利认为应该有。她说：“我不是科技怀疑主义者，我现在就在使用手机，刚刚查看了我的电子邮件——但我经常在一周的中间安排阅读假期：四、五个小时不上网。”

### Text interpretation

本文是一篇以讨论服饰功能为主题的文章，详细阐释了服饰的各种功能。在第一部分（第一至第二段），作者讲述了父亲参加二战的经历。父亲在诺曼底登陆战役中弹受伤，后来得以保留那件受伤时穿着的还留有弹孔的作战服。父亲说保存这件夹克是为了提醒自己当年是怎么差点就牺牲的。此部分是整篇课文的引子，通过讲述个人的亲身的经历，巧妙地引出文章的话题和主题：服装的常见功用和服装所承载的情感价值。在第二部分（第三至第十一段），作者阐述了从远古至今人类服装的各种功能。在远古时代，衣物是纯功能性的，作用是防寒保暖、遮羞蔽体。如今，服饰能够体现时尚潮流，深化印象，表达个性。此外，服装还具有另外两种更重要的功用，即表达情感和记录重要时刻。在大篇幅阐述服装的功能之后，作者在第十一段凝练出本文的主题：服装讲述我们的生活、经历与情感，象征我们对往昔纯真时代的怀念，提醒我们认清自己的身份，谨记我们是如何成为今天的自己的。文章第三部分（第十二段）回到父亲的那件旧夹克。作者一边抚摸着作战服上的弹孔，一边回味父亲那句没有说完的话。文章在此结束，留下丰富的品味空间。

教师可借助语篇中的词汇、句子进行隐性的课程思政。

从写作手法上看，本文最突出的特征是以一段故事开头，巧妙、生动、自然地引入话题，展开对服装功能的探讨。文章的结尾又回到故事本身，首尾呼应，使得文章更加连贯、完整。

### Culture notes

1 **D-Day**

In military usage, D-Day refers to a day on which a combat attack or operation starts or is scheduled to start. The most famous D-Day was on June 6, 1944, during World War II, when the Allies started the Normandy landings. The landings, launched simultaneously by the Allies on five separate beachheads in Normandy, France, involved bombing by air and sea, the landing of parachute troops, and the landing of soldiers and vehicles by sea. The operation marked the beginning of the liberation of France (and later Western Europe) from Nazi Germany. In general usage, D-Day can refer to a day when something important will happen, such as the launch of a new product.

2 **A beach in Normandy**

Normandy is a region in northern France, covering an area of about 30,000 km². The English Channel lies to the west and north. During the Normandy landings launched on June 6, 1944, around 50 miles (80 km) of Normandy's coast were targeted by Allied forces.

3 **A & E**

A & E is the abbreviation for accident and emergency department of a hospital, known in American English as the emergency room, or ER. This department specializes in emergency medicine, the care of patients who are injured or suddenly become ill and require immediate medical attention and treatment.

4 **Milan, Paris, London, and New York**

The four most prominent fashion capitals in the world in the 21st century. A fashion capital assumes a leadership role in fashion design, style creation, and the formation of fashion trends. The city's domestic and international profile is also related to its role as a fashion hub.

### Language points

1 **Been there, done that, got the T-shirt (Title)**

**Notes:** The phrase originally means to have experienced the topic under discussion to the point of boredom or complacency. People use this phrase if they are bored with something. This phrase began life in the early 1970s in the short form of "been there," and had the same meaning as "been there, done that," which first appeared in newspapers in 1982. About a decade later, "got the T-shirt" was added for extra emphasis. It wasn't long before people really could get the T-shirt. Such T-shirts, with this phrase printed as a slogan, have become a favorite of alumni associations worldwide. By using this slogan, the writer wanted to say that at a certain time in our life, we might buy clothes that suited a particular purpose or fashion (got the T-shirt), as in the literal meaning of the slogan. As the title of the passage, the slogan also indicates the main idea of the passage that clothes function as a reminder – at certain times in our lives we did a certain thing.

2 **... he would take the jacket out of the trunk in the attic, and show me the bullet hole. (Para. 2)**
**I would think about what he said, "Five centimetres to the right …" and I wouldn't be here today. (Para. 12)**

**Notes:** Here "would" is used to describe an action that somebody often or always did in the past. The author uses this structure in Paragraphs 2 and 12 to show how much his father valued and treasured the old battle jacket and how deeply he loved his father, thus highlighting the importance of the battle jacket in the author's family.

3 **... they needed to be warm and waterproof, and to protect our modesty. (Para. 3)**

**protect one's modesty:** hide your body or avoid body-related embarrassment 遮盖蔽体

*The dress showcases her beauty while still protecting her modesty.*

4 **This implies that there's nothing we can do about our climate, so we might as well bow to the inevitable and dress in warm clothes. (Para. 3)**

**Meaning:** This implies that we should wear warm clothes because the alternative (changing the weather) is impossible.

**might / may / could (just) as well:** (*infml.*) used to suggest doing sth. because you cannot think of anything better to do 倒不如

*Since it's raining heavily outside, we might just as well stay in and watch a movie.*

**bow to:** finally agree to do sth., even though you do not want to do it 屈服于；向……让步

*After much negotiation, he had to bow to their demands and accept the terms of the agreement.*

5 **... who are quite unlike most ordinary people. (Para. 4)**

**Meaning:** ... who are very different from ordinary people.

**Notes:** Confusingly, "quite" can be used to mean "fairly but not very," "very," or "completely," with the meaning determined by context or spoken stress. Here, it means "very" or "completely."

6 **They accordingly run the risk of inviting an unkind response. (Para. 4)**

**Meaning:** For that reason (inappropriate dressing style), those fashionistas are very likely to be ridiculed or criticized.

**run the risk (of doing sth. / of sth.):** be or put yourself in a situation in which sth. bad could happen to you 冒险（做某事）；冒……的危险

*If you don't double-check your work before submitting it, you run the risk of making errors that could negatively impact your grade.*

**dress down:** wear clothes that are more informal than the ones you usually wear （比平时）穿得随便

8 **Our wardrobes are largely filled with our favourites, because they make us feel good and fit for purpose. (Para. 6)**

**Meaning:** Most of the clothes in our wardrobes are our preferred choices, as they make us feel happy and suit our needs well.

9 **This is the other important function of fashion: a sense of occasion. (Para. 8)**

**a sense of occasion:** a feeling that a time or event is special or important 特殊场合的感觉；重要场合的感觉

*His performance was totally lacking any sense of occasion.*

10 **We wore them on a romantic first date which didn't work out. (Para. 8)**

**work out:** be successful or end in a particular way 成功；产生结果

*Despite initial challenges, their collaboration eventually worked out well, leading to a successful project.*

**highs and lows:** good times and bad times 人生的得意与失意；人生的跌宕起伏

### Analyzing writing techniques

**Reference answers**

1 The anecdote includes the following elements:

Time: In 1944 on the second day of the D-Day invasion
Place: A beach in Normandy, France
Character: My father
Object: A battle jacket

2 The anecdote is nostalgic.

3 The message the author intends to convey through the anecdote is that with time passes, clothes may serve different functions in different periods. The function of the father's battle jacket undergoes three stages of change:

- Stage 1 (on the second day of the D-Day invasion): an ordinary military uniform, maybe serving to indicate identity;
- Stage 2 (in the father's later life): a recorder of war adventures and a reminder of the impermanence of life;
- Stage 3 (in the author's life): a generator of fond nostalgia, representing the good old times or quality time spent with the father.

In summary, the battle jacket not only connects the father's past with the present, but also establishes a strong bond between the father and the son, and links the father's past with the author's present. It has become a tie that connects the whole family and an heirloom that will be passed down from generation to generation, carrying on the legacy of inheritance.

4 The story about the battle jacket ends with the author slipping his finger through the hole in the battle jacket and completing what the father had tactfully avoided mentioning by saying, "I wouldn't be here today." The author places the end of the story at the concluding part of the text to bring the text a natural ending, helping to achieve textual coherence and unity.

5 At the beginning of the text (Paragraphs 1–2), the battle jacket serves as a starter of the topic of the text that arouses the reader's interest. At the end of the text, it serves as a closure of the topic that brings the text to a natural ending. Throughout the text, it links the beginning and end of the text, helping to achieve textual coherence and unity.

2 ***Hanfu***

- **History**: It is the attire worn by the Han people from the reign of the Huangdi to the end of the Ming Dynasty. In recent years, there has been a revival of traditional Chinese attires.
- **Features**: A complete set of *hanfu* has three layers: an undergarment, an inner garment, and an overcoat. Various accessories, including socks, shawl, and belt complement the attire. *Hanfu* is distinguished by key features, including a cross-collar design with the left lapel overlaying the right, the use of sashes instead of buttons for fastening, and loose-fitting sleeves.
- **Functions / Significance**: *Hanfu* exerts a far-reaching influence on the clothing of neighboring countries, such as Japan and Vietnam.

***Cheongsam***

- **History**: The Cheongsam, which has its origin back in the 17th century, is recognized as the most famous traditional Chinese dress. It evolved from Manchu women's changpao ("long gown") of the Qing Dynasty.
- **Features**: The cheongsam is a one-piece garment with a fitted cut only worn by women. The cheongsam has undergone continuous improvements, and its typical style features a straight collar, a wide open placket on the right, a snug waist, and slits on both sides. It comes in both long-sleeved and short-sleeved variations.
- **Functions / Significance**: The cheongsam was popularized by upper-class women in Shanghai during the 1920s–1930s. Nowadays, it is recognized worldwide and has inspired many foreign adaptations because of its simple yet exotic lines. It is popular because it fits the Chinese female figure well. It has simple lines and looks elegant.

**The Zhongshan suit**

- **History**: The Zhongshan suit is a traditional formal attire for men in China. The main idea behind this garment is a combination of a Western-style suit and traditional Chinese clothing. It was first introduced by Dr. Sun Yat-sen as a form of national dress shortly after the foundation of the Republic of China. Today, some leaders still wear Zhongshan suits when attending major domestic events.
- **Features**: Zhongshan suits can be worn on formal and casual occasions because of their symmetrical shape, plain appearance, and sense of elegance and sedateness. The colors of Zhongshan suits are various, but usually plain, including black, white, blue, and grey. Wearers choose different colors for different situations.
- **Functions / Significance**: The suit is believed to hold strong symbolic meanings and cultural and political implications. The three smaller buttons on each cuff represent the Three Principles of the People (Nationalism, Democracy, the People's Livelihood). The four pockets on the front represent the four virtues of the Chinese culture: Propriety, Justice, Integrity, and Shame. The five big buttons on the front represent the five separate powers of "legislation, execution, jurisdiction, supervision, and examination." All these features together symbolize the unity of China.

领带

1 领带究竟是时尚配饰还是中小学男生的噩梦？历代英国的中小学男生（女生也一样）每天早上都要费一番力气打上校服领带，领带能打对就不错了，他们绝对不会想到领带跟时尚还有什么关系。但是，领带显然并不像外套或袜子那样是必不可少的，它既不蔽体，也不保暖。那么他们是如何变得如此常见，我们又为何喜欢佩戴领带呢？

2 大多数历史学家认为领带起源于 17 世纪。法国人雇佣克罗地亚士兵为其作战，其中一些士兵受到国王路易十四召见。在视察时，国王注意到他们每个人的军服上都有一块布系在脖子上。这块布用来系住上衣的领子，但也有不错的装饰效果，国王路易十四立刻喜欢上了这样的装束。事实上，他非常喜欢这种五颜六色的领带，并规定把它作为皇家集会的必备饰品。他称这种领带为"Cravat（领巾）"，取自"克罗地亚人（Croatians）"。

3 这种领巾在 18 世纪的法国风靡一时。但凡自认为是穿着考究的法国绅士都会炫耀自己的领巾。

4 19 世纪初，领结作为其前身领巾的改良款，进入了大众视野。领结很快成为"最潮流的"配饰，成为所有人的标志性饰品。当时每个带领结的人都尝试用各种面料、打结法和其他辅助性配饰将领结打造成属于自己的配饰。到 19 世纪 80 年代中期，领结已经成为具有时尚意识的男人衣橱里的主打配饰。

5 到了 20 世纪，男性时尚不再像先前那么重视外观，而是更加强调舒适性、功能性与合身，正式的领饰开始降温。随着翻领衬衫的发展，一种不同的、更长的领带出现了。20 世纪 20 年代，纽约一位名叫杰西·朗斯多夫的领带制造商申请了一种新的领带裁剪方法的专利。该方法从布料的一个角开始裁剪，然后将布料缝成三段，创造了朗斯多夫领带。这种裁剪方法使得领带具有了现代的外观，并创造出不同的打结方法。

6 在随后的几十年里，领带逐渐演变成我们今天所佩戴的样子。如今，领带在世界舞台上仍扮演重要角色，被誉为权力的象征，体现出人们对时尚的坚定追随。不打领带出席商务会议或政治峰会是在冒险。而且，只要国家领导人在重要场合坚持佩戴领带，我们大家（可以说）也会效仿。

循环时尚：可以拯救地球的购物理念

1 可持续性对购物者和时尚业来说从来都不重要。然而，我们这一代人更关心地球。二月份异常温暖的气温可能让我们倍感舒适，但我们知道气温异常的原因复杂不明。我们不能看着动物吃海洋里的塑料制品而窒息却无动于衷。我们认为每年有数百万吨的旧衣服未能回收而被丢弃是不可原谅的。

2 道德和生态友好型品牌的兴起已成为热议话题，但我们应该如何协调对时尚和服装的热爱与可持续性之间的关系呢？一个正在业内流传的较陌生的术语声称是解决问题的答案——循环时尚。2014 年，安娜·布里斯玛博士提出了这个术语，并就该话题帮助创建了好用的消费者指南。"循环时尚是一个比'可持续时尚'更宽泛的术语，因为循环时尚结合了可持续性和循环性的原则，"她说。"循环时尚可以被定义为：在服装、鞋子或配饰的设计、采购、生产和供应的每一个环节，都考虑到能够让其尽可能长时间地、负责任地、有效地在社会上使用和流通，然后在失去效用后，安全回归生物圈。"

3 循环时尚从设计阶段即已开始。在生产一件单品之前，首先要考虑设计的持久性，然后考虑其材料的可持续性。如果物品旧了，先不要丢弃，可以对其修补或重新设计，然后出租、交换或作为二手物品出售。无论哪种方式，都可以减少购买新物品，也可减少对地球的破坏。目前的时尚体系是线性的，因而需要彻底的改变。

4 可持续循环时尚的开拓者们共同努力，以巨大的奉献精神和创新精神挑战现状。设计界的后起之秀、2019 年"伊丽莎白二世女王英国设计奖"得主贝萨尼·威廉斯就是其中一员。这位伦敦的设计师使用可持续材料来设计她的街头服饰系列。她还与不同的慈善机构合作，积极帮助这些慈善机构，而不仅仅是捐款。她的目标是建立一个以英国为基地的生产单位，将循环服饰的生产做成一个环，而并非仅仅一条生产线。"我会把它当成一个培训项目，将它作为一个社会型企业来运营，"她说。"我会有不同的架构，我会有自己的品牌和批发业务，然后开展咨询、举办讲座并实施教学，以艺术家的身份举办展览。"

5 循环时尚的兴起让人们开始严格审视快时尚，随后转向更深思熟虑、更有道德意识的购物理念。我们对新奇事物的渴望，或者想在周五晚上穿新裙子的临时欲望，都是有代价的。衣服穿得次数越来越少，丢弃的速度越来越快。那么，高街时尚巨头们可能参与到循环时尚中吗？答案是肯定的。一些品牌已经开始研究可回收材料，在设计之初就考虑耐用性，之后会考虑在化学品和水管理方面进行可持续生产。他们还推出了店内消费者回收计划：购物者如果把衣服交还给店家以供回收，就可以得到一张代金券作为回报。对于这些品牌来说，要做到百分之百的循环，就意味着要在其价值链的每一个环节都采用循环方法。他们还需要在运营环节实现循环，包括从商店、办公室和物流中心，一直到运输。这样做可以确保这种循环方法将使公司最大限度地利用资源和减少浪费，减少对环境和社会造成的影响。

6 当然，鉴于生产可持续服装需要使用符合道德标准的材料和生产过程，它的价格较高，不是每个人都能支付得起的。然而，（即便）没有充足的预算，人们依旧可以过可持续的生活。最好的办法是不再追求快时尚，而是购买投资性单品。鼓励更多的人通过出售和购买来尝试使用二手物品，可以降低人们的花费。把旧衣服送给慈善商店和朋友，或者转售，这样就可以让这些旧衣服在被丢弃垃圾场之前尽可能长时间地为人们所用。

7 这种再次出售物品的想法并不新鲜，但却越来越受欢迎。十年前，二手市场遭到误解，得不到时尚界的认可。如今，这个市场已经成熟，可以满足人们对既经济实惠又可持续的时尚的需求。当然，时尚潮流的概念在时尚业仍有一席之地。同样，二手商品也顺应了当前的趋势。从风格的角度来看，将复古的二手单品与较新的单品混搭在一起似乎更具个性，更加时尚。

8 两种根深蒂固的观点对循环时尚提出质疑。第一，循环时尚只不过是回收的新说法。第二，快速时尚的衰落不利于商业发展。此观点认为，如果我们减少购物，销售量就会下降，就业机会就会减少。第一种观点很容易驳斥——回收只是新的循环时尚体系的众多组成部分之一。一件衣服是不是真正的循环型衣服，需要符合很多标准。至于第二种观点，则要看行业如何适应，而且需要尽快适应。

9 在这个耕地、淡水、矿物和石油资源有限的世界里，时尚产业必须找出新的经营方法。时尚产业应摒弃短期的营利经济模式，建立起长期的共享经济模式。

## How cities should prepare for artificial intelligence

### Text interpretation

人工智能时代一定会到来，并不会因为个人的意愿而改变。人工智能在给我们带来很多便利的同时也带来了挑战。怎样才能更好地利用并应对这些挑战，需要城市管理部门积极应对。

本文论述了城市应该怎样适应即将到来的人工智能时代并应对人工智能的挑战。文章分为两部分。第一部分（第一至第五段）首先明确城市在未来的人工智能时代扮演着重要角色，并且提出了为了适应人工智能时代，智慧城市所需要具备四项基本要素。

在介绍四项基本要素时，作者提到，城市应具备抓住新机遇的动力，并以杭州和深圳举例。在这里，教师可指出，中国正"加快转变超大城市发展方式，实施城市更新行动，加强城市基础设施建设，打造宜居、韧性、智慧城市。"引导学生关注中国智慧城市建设，实现隐形的课程思政教育。

第二部分（第六至第十二段），作者从评估当前人工智能科技对本地居民的冲击，协助本地居民做好准备，彻底改革教育体系三个方面，分别从城市管理部门和城市当地企业两个角度论述应如何应对人工智能时代的挑战。

这是一篇典型的议论文，作者按照提出问题——分析问题——解决问题的结构进行论述，结构清晰明朗。同时，作者在开头部分全面分析了在人工智能时代城市会遇到的问题，在论述之前提供了丰富的背景知识，让读者对问题有全面的了解；同时，在论述过程中引用了丰富事例来佐证观点。教师可结合课文理解练习部分和写作技能部分引导学生进行分析，更全面地了解文章的写作方式，同时让学生学会写观点类的议论文。

### Language points

1 While there is intense controversy about the ways artificial intelligence will continue to transform industries and organizations … (Para.1)

Meaning: People have different opinions on how AI will change industries and organizations.

controversy: n. [C, U] a serious argument about sth. that involves many people and continues for a long time （涉及很多人且历时很久的）争论，争议

The decision to build a new highway through the forest sparked a heated controversy among environmentalists and local residents.

2 Many cities have plans to become "smart cities" armed with AI-driven processes and services … (Para. 2)

Notes: "Process" here means "a series of actions used to make a product." For example,

The factory has a highly efficient process for assembling smartphones, involving multiple automated steps to ensure quality and precision.

3 Millions of people will need to find new professions as the nature of work is completely transformed, and will likely need assistance in transitioning to new jobs as they see the disappearance of their existing jobs. (Para. 2)

Notes: The sentence talks about the challenges that people may encounter since technology has changed the nature of work and people should be prepared for the transformation. Also, they will need help during the transition.

4 … a vision for responding to technological change … (Para. 3); Singapore and Dubai have detailed visions for how … (Para. 4)

Notes: Here, "vision" refers to "sb.'s idea or hope of how sth. should be done, or how it will be in the future." It can be followed by prepositions like "of" and "for." For example,

Simmons is willing to do anything to achieve his vision of social justice.

They outlined their vision for regenerating the old harbor district.

5 … they are examining ways to adopt new technologies and to recruit leading AI research labs and innovators. (Para. 4)

Notes: In this sentence, the word "recruit" has different meanings. In the expression "recruit leading AI research labs", it refers to "form a new army, team, etc. by persuading new people to join it【（通过招募）组成，组建】". In the expression "recruit innovators", it refers to "find new people to join a company, an organization, the armed forces, etc."

6 Administrations of European cities exceed expectations when it comes to being able to implement AI-related plans. (Para. 5)

exceed: vt. (fml.)

1 be more than a particular number or amount 超过；超出

The company's annual profits are expected to exceed last year's earnings.

2 do more than the law or an order, etc. allows you to do 超越（法律、命令等的）限制

She was told not to exceed the speed limit while driving on the highway.

when it comes to (doing) sth.: when it is a question of sth. 当涉及某事（或做某事）时

When it comes to choosing a vacation destination, I prefer places with beautiful natural landscapes.

7 Megalopolises like London, New York, and Paris have the rare trio of top talent, top employers, and top educational institutions, which should help propel themselves into an AI-driven future. (Para. 5)

Meaning: Major cities like London, New York, and Paris have a unique combination of top talent, employers, and educational institutions. This combination is expected to contribute to their success and growth in an AI-driven future.

8 So, what should cities and local companies do to set themselves up for success amid the coming shifts? (Para. 6)

Meaning: So, what should cities and local companies do to ensure their success in the face of the challenges posed by AI?

set sb. up: put sb. in a position in which they are able to do sth., or in which sth. is likely to happen to them 使某人处于某种位置；把某人置于某种境地

By acquiring essential skills and building a strong network, individuals can set themselves up for success.

9 But cities might approach this concern by conducting timely and thoughtful workforce analysis and forging public-private partnerships. (Para. 6)

Notes: It is a general statement, outlining what cities should do. Paragraphs 7 and 8 further explain the statement by separately discussing how cities should do workforce analysis and how they should forge public-private partnerships.

10 … and what skills will be in high demand going forward. (Para. 7)

going forward: in the near future – used esp. in business and economic contexts 将来，今后（尤为商业、经济用语）

Going forward, we will be able to deliver better products to our customers.

11 At the other end of the spectrum, city administrations should assist with re-skilling and development efforts for the local community, so that residents and employees can find new opportunities when necessary. (Para. 9)

Notes: The word "spectrum" refers to "a scale that represents different positions, values, or options." "At the other end of the spectrum" here represents a significantly different viewpoint.

12 One way to achieve this is for cities to appoint chief digital officers to play this role, as has been done in New York, Melbourne, Boston, London, and Athens. (Para. 9)

Notes: In this sentence, "as" is a conjunction and is used to make a comment or to add information about what the author has just said.

A chief digital officer (CDO) or a chief digital information officer (CDIO) is an individual who helps a company, a government organization, or a city drive growth by converting traditional "analog" businesses to digital ones using the potential of modern online technologies and data (i.e., digital transformation), and at times oversees operations in the rapidly changing digital sectors like mobile applications, social media and related applications, virtual goods, as well as web-based information management and marketing.

13 New skills gained from job exchange programs can help people rapidly resettle and possibly land even more rewarding jobs. (Para. 10)

Meaning: People can find a new job quickly and even succeed in getting a fulfilling job with the help of the skills gained from job exchange programs.

Notes: "Land" here is used as a verb, meaning "get a job."

14 The transition to an AI future leaves no room for delay. (Para. 12)

room (– for sth.); n. [U] the possibility of sth. existing or happening; the opportunity to do sth. 可能性；机会

There's some room for improvement in your work.

## Should robots be given the same rights as humans in the future?

### Language points

1 Increasingly, people are primed to see robots as beyond mere scraps of metal and plastic. (Para. 1)

Meaning: People are more likely to see robots as those who resemble human beings rather than what is composed of materials.

prime

vt. prepare sb. for a situation so that they know what to do 使准备好（应付某一情况）

(– sb. to do sth.) The inspirational speech by the coach primed the team to perform at their best during the championship game.

(be primed for sth.) The riot police were primed for action.

a. (only before noun) most important 最重要的；主要的

Winning is not the prime objective in this sport.

beyond: more than sth. 超出；除……之外

Our success was far beyond what we thought possible.

2 An even more compelling reason to grant human rights to robots is the protection of societal values. (Para. 3)

Notes: The structure "An even more …" is used to introduce another supporting point. It serves the same purpose as "in addition," "moreover," and "what's more," but it presents the point more strongly.

3 Moreover, the resultant impact of granting rights to robots may prove to be catastrophic to mankind. (Para. 5)

**未来应该赋予机器人与人类同样的权利吗？**

1 名叫索菲亚的人形机器人最近被授予沙特阿拉伯的公民身份。人们越来越倾向于不仅仅把机器人视为金属和塑料碎片的组合。相反，人们鼓励将机器人视为类人。因此，有些人认为机器人应该得到法律和道德上的保护。

2 他们认为，伤害机器人的同时也可能伤害人类自己。由于人类可能会向他们的机器人同伴透露私人细节，存储在机器人中的数据就有可能被上传到云端。私营机构可以访问这些保存在机器人云端的敏感信息。在这种情况下，为机器人提供法律和道德上的保护实际上有助于保护人类自己。

3 赋予机器人人权的另一个更令人信服的理由是保护社会价值观。机器人看起来像真人，应该强烈的反对和谴责施加在他们身上的任何暴力或破坏行为。纵容对机器人的暴力会导致纵容对人类的暴力。

4 但是，也有一些人认为不应该赋予机器人人权。他们认为机器人仍然缺乏能够像人类一样思考、行动和感知的心智机能。通常，机器人只不过是按照计算机程序自动执行命令的机器。机器人按照程序客观地实现目标，无法评估其行动可能对人类造成的损害。机器人不是有感觉的生物，因此不能像人类一样感知特定的情境或处理主观情况。

5 此外，授予机器人人权可能会对人类造成灾难性的后果。毫无戒备之心的机器人很容易受到网络攻击。在感染病毒时，能够获取敏感、机密信息的机器人很可能会威胁人类生命或者国家安全。如果机器人被授予与人类相同的权利，这种情况将进一步恶化。交换病毒爆发的关键数据可能需要拆卸机器人，因此数据交换可能会被禁止，因为拆卸机器人侵犯了机器人的“人权”。

6 总之，决定机器人是否应该享有人权绝非易事。但是随着机器人在我们日常生活中的普及，我们必须在讨论是否赋予机器人人权之前，优先确保机器人能够得到妥善应用。我们需要识别和解决出现的安全问题，为此，定期测试和维护非常重要。建立明确的机器人使用政策和规定可以确保机器人按规定行事。

3 然而，尽管这些创新具有重大意义，但它们并没有改变人类行为的主要方面，也就是我所说的“社交套件”。社交套件可以归纳为我们在数十万年中发展出的一系列至关重要的能力，包括爱、友谊、合作和教育。这些能力的本质在全世界是一致的，不管人们是在城市还是农村，不管他们是否使用现代技术。

4 但是在人类社会中使用人工智能可能会更具破坏性。特别是当机器被制造成外貌和行为都与人类相似，并深深地融入我们的生活时，它们很可能会让我们变得不那么友爱、善良。

5 在耶鲁大学的实验室，我和我的同事们一直在探索这种破坏性是如何产生的。在其中一个实验中，我们指导几小组人与人形机器人一起在虚拟世界中铺设铁轨。每组包括三个人和一个蓝白相间的小机器人，他们坐在一张方桌旁，在平板电脑上工作。预设的程序会让机器人时不时地犯错，并认错：“对不起，伙计们，我这一轮犯了错，” 它开心地说，“我知道这难以置信，但机器人也会犯错。”

6 事实证明，这个笨拙、坦率、勇于认错的机器人却帮助小组成员表现得更好，因为它增强了人与人之间的交流。人们变得更放松、更健谈，安慰那些犯错的小组成员，并且比以往更经常地一起开怀大笑。而对照组中的机器人只是平淡地陈述事实。与对照组相比，有勇于承认错误的机器人的实验组成员之间合作得更好。

7 在另一个虚拟实验中，我们将 4,000 名人类受试者分成约 20 人一组的小组，并在小组中给每个人分配一个“朋友”：这些友谊形成了社交网。然后我们给各组都分配了任务。每个人都必须从三种颜色中选择一种，但他们的颜色不能与其社交网中指定的朋友的颜色相同。受试者不知道，一些小组中有几个机器人会按照设定的程序偶尔犯错。直接挨着这些机器人的人变得更加灵活。他们也倾向于避免陷入对某个人有效但对整个群体无效的解决方案。更重要的是，由此产生的灵活性在整个社交网中传播，甚至影响到那些没有直接挨着机器人的人。因此，有犯错机器人的组总是比没有犯错的机器人的组表现得更好。机器人有助于人类自己帮助自己。

8 但我们也不应低估人工智能的负面影响。在人类社会环境中使用人工智能会降低我们的生产力，而且产生更加不道德的行为。另一项实验旨在探索人工智能如何影响“公众悲剧”，即个人以自我为中心的行为可能会损害集体的共同利益。在一项网络游戏中，我们发给数千名受试者够多轮游戏使用的资金。在每一轮游戏中，我们告知受试者，他们可以保留自己的钱，也可以将部分或全部钱捐赠给邻居。如果他们选择捐赠，我们会再提供等额资金给他们的邻居，让他们的邻居的钱翻倍。游戏刚开始时，三分之二的受试者都表现得很慷慨。他们意识到，在某一轮中对邻居大方可能会促使邻居在下一轮中对他们大方。然而，从自私和短视的角度来看，最好的结果是留着自己的钱并从邻居那里得到钱。在这项实验中我们发现，通过添加几个表现自私的机器人（冒充人类玩家），我们可以让团队成员做出类似的行为。最终，人类玩家完全停止了合作。于是，这些机器人把一群慷慨的人变成了自私的麻烦制造者。

9 让我们停下来思考一下这一发现的含义。合作是人类的一个关键特征，对社会生活至关重要。信任和慷慨是区分成功组和失败组的关键。如果每个人都为了帮助团队而积极参与并作出自我牺牲，每个人都会受益。然而，随着这种行为模式遭到破坏，公共利益的概念消失了，每个人都受到了损害。人工智能可能会严重降低人类的合作能力，这一点令人非常担忧。

10 启蒙运动哲学家托马斯·霍布斯认为，人类需要一个集体协议，防止我们变得混乱、残忍。他错了。早在我们组建政府之前，人类就通过进化形成了一组社会套件，让我们能够和平、有效地生活在一起。在前人工智能世界，基因中传承下来的爱、友谊、合作和教育等各种能力一直帮助人类作为一个整体社会生活在一起。

11 可惜的是，由于时间紧迫，人类未能进化出类似的与机器人相处的遗传能力。因此，我们必须采取措施，确保它们能够与我们友好地生活在一起。随着人工智能更充分地融入我们的生活，我们可能还需要一种新的社会契约——与机器而不是与其他人 的社会契约。

## China's underwater history

### Text interpretation

本文用严谨的语言讨论了全球化的历史以及中国海上丝绸之路在其中的作用。"南海一号"沉船考古发掘项目，展现了国家水下考古的科技创新，复现了中国海上丝绸之路的繁盛，更加激励学生们乘着传递和平与友谊的"文化方舟"，航行于21世纪海上丝绸之路，讲好中国故事、传播好中国声音，展现可信、可爱、可敬的中国形象，推动中华文化更好走向世界。

在本文的第一、二段，作者提出将贸易的全球化仅归功于15世纪开始的西欧探险家是不公平的，13世纪和14世纪的丝绸之路对贸易全球化也有重要贡献。除了陆上丝绸之路这条高效的文化交流和商业贸易路线，海上丝绸之路在全球历史中也举足轻重。接着在文章的第三至第六段，作者以中国东南海岸城市泉州为例，通过讨论其在历朝快速增长的海上贸易，帮助读者了解海上丝绸之路的重要意义。早在唐朝时期，泉州就已经拥有一支完善的海船舰队。到了元朝时期，泉州已经成为东亚最大的海港，船只从这里出发，经常沿着今天被称为海上丝绸之路的海外贸易路线航行。从当时茶叶在全世界的流行可窥见通过海上丝绸之路进行的全球性贸易。然而为什么海上丝绸之路长久以来一直不为大众所熟知呢？文章的第七至第十一段给出答案。作者基于"南海一号"的考古勘察以及另一艘唐代船只的考古发现提出，随着水下考古技术和技巧的成熟，我们将会对海上丝绸之路有更深刻的认识，也将揭示长期以来被欧洲中心主义历史所掩盖的全球历史的真相。

全文在一开始就提出了明确的学术观点，即沿着海上丝绸之路进行的中国海上贸易在全球历史中有着重要的意义。就学术观点和思想内容而言，本文带领读者探讨了被欧洲中心主义历史观掩盖的全球历史的真相。就写作结构和特点而言，本文有效地采用了多种论据，按照时间顺序，罗列、分析论据，通过翔实的论据，缜密的逻辑，发展并支持了中心论点。教师可以结合理解练习和写作策略引导学生注意这种写作特点，并在 Sharing your ideas 部分尝试使用这种策略。同时，在课文中出现了大量和历史有关的词汇与表达，教师可以引导学生在语言练习中夯实这些词汇和表达，并在产出任务中有效使用。

### Culture notes

**The Tang Dynasty Shipwreck**

Called "黑石号" in Chinese, it is the wreck of an Arabian ship that sank around 830 AD. The ship completed the outward journey from Arabia to China, but sank on the return journey from China, approximately 1.6 kilometers off the coast of Belitung Island, Indonesia. Among the items discovered on board, there was a never-before-seen Tang mirror, whose inscription identifies it as one of the famed specimens cast on the Yangzi River in Yangzhou, a so-called Jiangxin Mirror (江心镜).

### Language points

1 **There's some truth to this narrative.** (Para. 1)

**Meaning:** The account is partly true.

**there is some truth to / in sth.:** it is at least partly true 某事至少部分是真的

*While the story might be exaggerated, there is some truth to his claims about the challenges he faced during his journey.*

**Notes:** The structure "there is some truth to ..." is often used when the writer or speaker is going to introduce a new statement opposing to the original narrative.

2 **It is, however, unjust to frame the globalization of trade as exclusively the work of ...** (Para. 1)

**frame ... as ... :** regard ... as ... ; describe ... as ... 把……当成；把……说成

*It is ridiculous that the liar framed himself as a hero who saved the whole village.*

3 **... that globalization has its roots in the 13th and 14th centuries.** (Para. 2)

**have (one's) roots in ... :** have sth. as a primary source or origin 根源在于……；原因在于……

*The high crime rate in this big city has its roots in high unemployment and extreme poverty.*

4 **... that was famed for bringing Marco Polo to the Yuan court.** (Para. 2)

**be famed for ... :** be well-known for ... 以……著称；以……闻名

*The Netherlands is famed for a variety of cheese.*

5 **Quanzhou was home to a well-built fleet of sea vessels as early as the Tang Dynasty.** (Para. 3)

**be home to:** be a place where sth. is commonly found 是……的所在地

*The wetland is home to a variety of wildlife, especially birds.*

**fleet:** *n.* [C]

1) a group of ships, or all the ships in a navy 船队；舰队

*A fleet of fishing boats was blocking the entrance to the port.*

2) a group of vehicles that are controlled or owned by one company 车队

*Survivors were taken to hospital in a fleet of ambulances.*

**vessel:** *n.* [C] (*fml.*) a ship or large boat 船；舰

*A warning went out to fishing vessels in the region.*

6 **Their creators made use of sophisticated carpentry techniques not dissimilar from those ...** (Para. 3)

**not dissimilar from:** not different from; similar to 和……没有不同

*The problems that we face today are not dissimilar from those of 30 years ago.*

7 **These techniques allowed the creation of independent, watertight cabins capable of containing flooding at sea.** (Para. 3)

**Meaning:** The techniques allowed for the construction of cabins that were capable of keeping water from spreading beyond their boundaries, effectively preventing widespread flooding and maintaining the ship's integrity in case of a breach or accident.

8 **... a collection of overseas trade routes that arguably reached its peak during the Yuan Dynasty.** (Para. 5)

**reach its peak:** come to the point when sb. / sth. is best, most successful, strongest, etc. 达到顶峰

*The sales of chocolate reached its peak during the Christmas season.*

9 **The salvage work was suspended for ten years due to the constraints of science, technology, and economic power.** (Para. 9)

**salvage:**

*n.* [U] when you save things from a situation in which other things have already been damaged, destroyed, or lost （对财物等的）抢救

*The salvage of the sunken ship's cargo was a complex operation that required specialized equipment and expertise.*

*v.* save sth. from an accident or bad situation in which other things have already been damaged, destroyed, or lost （从事故或糟糕情形中）抢救出（某物）

*The crew worked tirelessly to salvage as much equipment as possible from the fire-damaged building.*

**suspend:** *vt.* officially stop sth. for a time; prevent sth. from being active, used, etc. for a time 暂停；中止

*The government has decided to suspend production at the country's biggest lead plant.*

10 **This suggests that the ship may have been part of an international industrial chain with a sophisticated division of labor, involving multiple stops along the Maritime Silk Road, each responsible for a different step in the manufacturing process.** (Para. 9)

**Notes:** It can be inferred from the archeological findings that there existed a complex system in which different places were in charge of different work. In this sense, the different cities along the Maritime Silk Road where the fleet stopped contributed to the production of goods and they together formed the network of the international industry at that time.

11 **They will also reveal the truth about global history that has long been buried by more Eurocentric accounts of the past.** (Para. 11)

**Meaning:** The archeological findings will uncover the facts about global history and provide evidence to support the viewpoint against Eurocentric stereotypes about global history.

## The story of the Silk Road (Video)

### Scripts

More than 2,000 years ago, the people of Eurasia explored and opened up several routes of trade and cultural exchange, which were called the "Silk Road" by later generations. The Silk Road helps to explain many mysteries of ancient China. Chinese tea was once sold abroad with the name of "oriental leaves." Exquisite porcelains and silks were introduced to Southeast and South Asia, and even reached the distant Arabian world, Europe, and the east coast of Africa. Meanwhile, precious spices, pearls, and colored glazes were brought back to China.

In 1983, a 2,000-year-old silver box in the Persian style was unearthed in the Chinese city of Guangzhou, a reflection of the impact of the Silk Road. There are more legends to be told. In 671 A.D., the monk Yijing commenced an epic sea voyage from Guangzhou, and eventually returned to China from India with over 400 Buddhist scriptures. In 1582, an Italian priest, Matteo Ricci, later praised as "the first person to link Chinese and Western cultures," came to China. He introduced contemporary Western science, technology, and art.

When President Xi Jinping visited Central Asia and Southeast Asia in 2013, he raised the idea of jointly building the Silk Road Economic Belt and the 21st-Century Maritime Silk Road, also referred to as the Belt and Road Initiative. This initiative has attracted close attention from all over the world. Since then, transportation networks have been improved, new ports have been built, and investment and energy projects have been studied or completed along the Belt and Road. For example, the first stage of the Jatigede Dam Project was jointly built by China and Indonesia. It now has the capacity to irrigate 90,000 hectares of farmland, benefiting 4.8 million local people and creating more than 2,000 jobs.

If all countries along the Belt and Road make concerted efforts to pursue a common goal, there will be bright prospects for the Silk Road Economic Belt and the 21st-Century Maritime Silk Road. The people of countries along the Belt and Road will all benefit from this initiative, as they once did from the ancient Silk Road.

### Background information

The Belt and Road Initiative (BRI) is a development strategy and framework that focuses on connectivity and cooperation among countries primarily between China and the rest of Eurasia. The name was coined in 2013 by President Xi Jinping. The "Belt" refers to the Silk Road Economic Belt, a land-based network of transportation and infrastructure projects connecting China with Central Asia, Russia, and Europe. The "Road" refers to the 21st Century Maritime Silk Road, a sea-based network connecting China with Southeast Asia, South Asia, Africa, and Europe. Together, the Belt and Road Initiative aims to promote regional integration and connectivity along both the land-based and sea-based routes, ultimately facilitating cross-border trade and investment and promoting economic growth in the participating regions.

### Culture notes

1 Yijing

Yijing (635–713 A.D.) was a Chinese Buddhist monk who traveled to India in 671 A.D. and studied at the famous Buddhist University of Nalanda. He spent around ten years in India, studying with many famous Buddhist teachers and collecting a large number of Buddhist texts, which he later translated into Chinese. After returning to China, he continued his work of translating and promoting Buddhist texts, which had a significant impact on the development of Buddhism. Yijing's travels to India and his translation work can be seen as part of the wider historical context of the Silk Road, which facilitated cultural exchanges and the spread of ideas between China, India, and other regions.

2 Matteo Ricci

Matteo Ricci (1552–1610) was an Italian Jesuit priest and missionary. He is most famous for his work as a missionary in China, where he learned the language and customs of the people and used this knowledge to promote Christianity. Ricci also made important contributions to the fields of cartography and astronomy, creating maps of the world and introducing Western scientific concepts to Chinese scholars. His efforts to bridge the cultural divide between China and the West had a lasting impact and helped to shape the relationship between the two regions in the centuries that followed.

3 The Jatigede Dam

The Jatigede Dam is a large dam on the Cimanuk River in West Java, Indonesia. The dam was built to provide irrigation water for agriculture, as well as to generate hydroelectric power. Its construction began in 2008 and was completed in 2015. The construction of the Jatigede hydropower station began in 2015 and finished in 2019. The project received funding from the Export-Import Bank of China and involved the participation of Chinese companies in its construction. The project, which is part of the Belt and Road Initiative, aimed to boost economic development and trade by promoting infrastructure investment in Indonesia and Southeast Asia.

### Culture notes

1 the Zimmermann Note

The Zimmermann Note was a secret message sent by the German Foreign Secretary to the Mexican government proposing a military alliance between Germany and Mexico in the event that the U.S. entered World War I on the side of the Allies. The note suggested that Mexico should invade the U.S. and in return, Germany would provide military and financial support to Mexico. The note was intercepted and deciphered by British intelligence, and its publication in American newspapers in March 1917 played a significant role in the decision of the U.S. to enter the war.

2 the Mexican War

The Mexican War was a conflict between the U.S. and Mexico from 1846 to 1848. The war was triggered by various factors, including the annexation of Texas to the United States in 1845, as well as disputes over the boundary between Texas and Mexico. The war ended with the signing of the Treaty of Guadalupe Hidalgo. The treaty established the Rio Grande as the boundary between Texas and Mexico, and Mexico ceded a vast territory to the U.S. In return, the U.S. paid Mexico $15 million and assumed the claims of American citizens against Mexico, which amounted to $3.25 million.

3 the American Civil War

The American Civil War was a major conflict in the U.S. from 1861 to 1865 between the Union (Northern states) and the Confederacy (Southern states) over issues including states' rights, slavery, and territorial expansion. The war began when Southern states seceded from the Union and formed the Confederacy, and it ended with the surrender of Confederate forces and the abolition of slavery. The war officially began on April 12, 1861, when Confederate forces attacked Fort Sumter, a Union-held fort in South Carolina. The Civil War was one of the deadliest conflicts in American history, resulting in the loss of over 600,000 lives and significant social, political, and economic changes.

4 到了元代，泉州已成为东亚最大的海港。在约 1346 年，著名旅行家、旅行作家伊本·白图泰乘船抵达泉州。他后来描述了亲眼所见的泉州港口沿岸的盛况，数百艘大船和成千上万艘小船停在港口，其中一些能够运载多达 1,000 名乘客。

5 这些船只经常沿着今天被称为海上丝绸之路的海外贸易路线往返，该贸易路线在元朝时期达到鼎盛。这条线路从中国南部的泉州和广州开始，沿着印度洋进入红海，将草药和财宝带回中国，同时，将精美的商品和布料运往远东和地中海地区。

6 茶叶的全球传播就证明了当时贸易的繁荣。泉州是中国茶叶产地，最初，泉州的航海者航行时会携带大量的茶叶。不过，这些茶叶并不是为了交易，而是为了预防疾病。逐渐地，来自欧亚大陆各地的人们爱上了这种饮料，尽管他们经常喝的是改良的茶饮。今天仍然可以从英语单词 "tea" 看出茶叶的起源，它来源于中国汉字中 "茶" 的泉州方言发音。

7 海上丝绸之路对欧亚贸易网络的崛起有着举足轻重的作用，但为什么，特别是与陆上路线相比时，海上丝绸之路基本被遗忘了呢？部分原因在于海底考古的难度大。

8 然而这一情况正在发生改变。1987 年，在中国南部省份广东省阳江市海岸附近发现了一艘宋代的大型沉船。两年后，这艘沉船被命名为 "南海一号"，由中国历史博物馆和日本的亚洲水中考古学研究所联合勘测。这次行动初步确定了沉船的位置，标志着中国水下考古的开始。

9 但是沉船打捞仍面临巨大挑战。由于科学技术和经济实力有限，打捞工作被暂停了十年。2001 年，从海底打捞起大约 4,000 件物品。然而，直到 2007 年，考古学家们才得以实施大规模的操作，将整个沉船从水中打捞出来。除了大量珍贵的宋代瓷器外，考古学家们还在船上发现了许多精美的金饰品，其中包括一条 1.72 米长的波斯风格金色腰带，以及一个重 200 克的金手镯。更有趣的是，船上还有许多未装饰的金首饰。这表明该船可能是国际化的产业链的一环。该产业链具有复杂的分工，海上丝绸之路有多个停靠点，每个停靠点负责制造过程的不同环节。

10 "南海一号" 考古团队的负责人崔勇将这艘船称为 "时间胶囊"，它保存了宋代海上贸易的真实状态。"南海一号" 绝不是海上丝绸之路上唯一的 "时间胶囊"，还有许多类似的沉船沉睡在海底。1998 年，在印度尼西亚海岸发现了一艘唐代时期的沉船。后来的考古工作发掘出 67,000 多件瓷器，以及世界上唯一现存的唐代江心镜。

11 水下考古技术的成熟让我们对海上丝绸之路有更为清楚、深入的了解。水下考古发现不仅对泉州或中国的历史具有重要意义，而且能够揭示长期以来被欧洲中心主义的历史观所掩盖的全球历史的真相。

## Further exploration

丝绸之路上的中亚

1 纵观历史，交流和贸易路线在欧亚大陆上纵横交错，逐渐形成了今天的丝绸之路。从中国的长安（今西安）出发，丝绸之路分为三个方向和众多分支。丝绸之路的一条主要路线沿着河西走廊，经过塔里木盆地和帕米尔高原，进入中亚、伊朗、伊拉克、地中海东岸，一直延伸到欧洲的贸易中心。而中亚是这条线路上至关重要的一环。

2 在中亚，丝绸之路沿途散布着现代物流中心的原型——商队旅馆。随着丝绸之路沿线交通的增加，商队旅馆也越建越多。商队旅馆不仅为商人提供了固定的食宿场所，让他们为安全出行做好准备，而且也是让他们以物易物、在当地市场交易以及与其他商人旅客会面的场所。许多商人并没有沿着丝绸之路从中国一路走到欧洲，而是从事中转贸易。

3 古代城市不仅成为了中转贸易的中心，也发展成著名商品的生产地。例如，公元前二世纪，当丝绸之路初步形成时，生活在今天费尔干纳盆地的人们就已培育出了一种高大强壮的马，这种马据说会流汗成血。在接下来的几个世纪里，对中亚这种强壮、高大的马匹的需求成为了丝绸之路网络的主要经济驱动力之一。在中世纪早期，塔什干成为中亚的大型矿业和冶金区，为丝绸之路贸易提供了非常重要的贵金属、铁矿石和宝石。

4 历史学家认为，中亚便利的地理位置促使其成为移民和文化交流中心。经由丝绸之路，各种活动得以开展，外交条约和联盟得以缔结。中亚人民极大地促进了中国发明创造的传播，广为人知的有汉代发明的纸张、六世纪发明的印刷术的传播。此外，他们还将字母系统和书写系统、宗教以及许多文化和技术成就带到了东方。

5 丝绸之路蜿蜒曲折，从古代延续至今。今天，中亚的交通路线再次成为焦点。在 "一带一路" 倡议下，对铁路和公路的投资促进了大规模的交通基础设施升级，提高了中亚跨境和国内运输的竞争力。中亚国家也在打破贸易和旅行的障碍。更好的连通性和大规模的投资将使该地区在全球贸易中占据重要份额。

## Extended reading

为什么历史学家们意见不一

1 大多数学生都很欣赏专业历史学家能够从过去收集大量 "事实" 的能力。但是当他们发现即使面对同一个历史事件，历史学家们的意见却常常完全相左时，常常倍感困惑。

2 对于这种情况，他们常见的反应是：这个历史学家是正确的，那个历史学家弄错了，而那些历史学家犯错大概也是因为把 "事实" 搞错了。但是大多数情况并非如此。通常历史学家都言之有据，令人信服。那些 "事实" ——名字、日期、事件、数字——通常不会搞错。此外，学生们常常会发现观点相左的历史学家在 "事实" 方面意见基本一致。他们之所以得出不同的结论，是因为他们看待过去的角度有所不同。历史看起来似乎就是记住一些死板的 "事实" 而已，现在却变成了从众多的解释中选择一个好的解释。

3 要弄清历史学家们为什么会意见不一，学生们必须思考一个他们或多或少想当然的问题：他们必须自问历史到底是什么。

4 从最为广义的角度来看，历史是人类过去的总和。稍微狭义点的观点认为历史是被记录下来的过去，也就是说人类生活被以某种形式记录下来的那些部分，包括民间传说、物品或文档。最后，历史可以被定义为历史学家对过去的描述。当然这三个定义是相互关联的。历史学家的描述必须建立在前人留下的过去的遗存之上。显然他们无法了解所有的一切，原因很简单，因为不是所有的事件和所有发生的事情都被充分、完整地记录下来。所以历史学家最多只能推测和大致还原历史。

5 但这还不是全部。如果说因为不是所有的历史事件都被记录下来了，所以历史学家不能知晓一切，那么其实他们也不会把他们所能获取的所有记录都用于研究。相反，他们只会选取那些他们认为最为重要的历史记录。此外，他们还会重建部分历史。

6 历史学家在选取和提供证据的时候可能需要运用某些关于人类动机和行为的理论。有的时候这似乎很简单，不需要太多复杂和微妙的东西。比如，在研究美国加入一战的原因时，历史学家们会认为美国商船被德国潜艇击沉这个事件与他们的研究相关，而美国总统伍德罗·威尔逊 1917 年初买了顶自己不太满意的帽子这件事，就肯定不可能被他们当作证据。

7 如果所有的选择都这么简单，问题也就迎刃而解了。但做出选择没有那么容易。那些研究美国加入一战的历史学家会发现，除了德国潜艇战之外，还有其他一系列的史实可能与他们研究的事件相关。比如，他们会发现为了赢得美国公众对英国参战的支持，英国的宣传机器在美国开足了马力。他们会发现美国的银行家向英国提供了大笔的贷款，万一英国战败，这些贷款将得不到偿还。历史学家们会去解读被截获的 "齐默尔曼电报"，电报中德国外交部长建议促成德墨联盟，这样墨西哥在德国的支持下可以夺回在墨西哥战争中被美国占领的领土。历史学家还会发现很多美国参议员和其他政治领袖对欧洲的力量均衡非常关注，而如果德国击败法国和英国，成为欧洲唯一的霸主，欧洲的力量均衡将被打破，这对美国是很不利的。

8 那么历史学家该如何看待这些事实呢？一些历史学家会避免得出结论，仅把这些事实罗列出来。这么做的话，他们实际上做出了两个重要的论断：第一，他们列出的事实是美国参战的主要理由，那些没有被他们列出的事实并不重要；第二，在解释美国在一战中的作用时，他们所列出的这些事实是同等重要的。而另外一些历史学家则会指出这个清单并不完整，因此不具备有效性。例如，它忽略了一个事实：因为背景和教育的关系，伍德罗·威尔逊的立场总体上总是亲英的。这样一来，两派历史学家就产生了分歧。

9 从上述的例子中可以看到，历史学家意见不一不是因为他们的研究是从不同的理论前提出发的。但是另一个产生分歧的地方是由一个完全不同的原因引发的——有的时候历史学家之所以意见不一，是因为他们并不是在探讨同一件事情，通常他们只是在讨论事情因果的不同层面。如果一个老师说："美国内战爆发是因为在 1861 年 4 月 12 日，南卡罗来纳州的炮台向萨姆特的联邦要塞开火。" 我们不能因为这个说法不够准确就说它不对，但它不足以充分解释该事件。那么接下来要问的问题显而易见。为什么一个州和联邦会水火不容，必须通过战争来解决矛盾？换句话说，我们不能只停留在近因上面，必须要不断地深挖。但是随着我们对这个问题挖掘的越来越深，答案也变得越来越难，越来越复杂。最后，你可以把美国内战爆发的原因追溯到美洲大陆的发现，但是这是必要原因，不是充分原因。所以历史学家在关于从哪儿开始分析这一点上又会有意见分歧。

10 说到这里，现在学生们该明白了，广为流传的那句话 "让事实说话" 并没有什么实质意义。事实不会自己说话，历史学家以特定的方式来使用事实，所以说话的是历史学家而不是事实。
